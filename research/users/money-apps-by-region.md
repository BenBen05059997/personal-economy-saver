# What money apps are actually used for, by region

Researched 3 Nov 2026. Web only. Labels: [measured] = regulator, filing, audited or first-party log data; [community] = app-store reviews, forum posts, small surveys; [vendor] = company marketing or self-reported claims; [inference] = my reading. Every claim carries a URL and a year. Quotes are 15 words or fewer. App-store review quotes come from the public Apple RSS feeds (itunes.apple.com/{country}/rss/customerreviews/id={id}), read 3 Nov 2026, so the year is 2026 unless noted. Several figures reached me through search-engine summaries and were not re-read on the source page; those are marked "(summary)". Reddit was not reachable through search; no Reddit claim is made.

## Summary (10 lines)

1. The jobs that win are small and dated: "what leaves my account before I am paid again" and "who owes whom". Budgets and pie charts lose; reminders, sorting on payday, and settling up win.
2. Chime measured it: 49% of active members use SpotMe in a month and 26% use MyPay early pay, against 37% for Credit Builder [measured, S-1, 2025]. Paying people early beats budgeting.
3. Salary sorting and bills pots work as a sorting rule on income arrival (Monzo Salary Sorter, Qapital "payday divy", Toss locked savings, Nubank Caixinhas) [vendor+community, 2025-2026].
4. Autopay is half-trusted. KUBRA: 23% use it for all bills, 59% for some, 18% never; the non-users want to review first (67%) and control timing (58%) [vendor survey, 2025]. People living paycheck to paycheck use it half as much [PYMNTS, 2025].
5. Autopay fails on thin balances. In India, autopay revocations hit 20 million a month on low balance [press headline, 2025]. A warning before the debit is the unmet job.
6. Bill splitting is a real, measured habit: Monzo logged 8.8m bill splits from 10.1m requests in a year [vendor, 2025]; Tricount users split £20.2bn in 2025 [vendor, 2026]. People delete the app over notifications and paywalls.
7. Overdraft-style bridges are the biggest revenue jobs: Fuliza 9.1m customers and KES 629bn in six months [measured, Safaricom via press, 2025]; Dave $2.0bn ExtraCash in one quarter [measured, 2025]. Suggestions here must stay information, never a product push.
8. Cross-border earners use the account for receiving and converting, then withdraw locally (Payoneer into GCash; Wise; Grey). Speed and certainty of arrival is the cited pain: 41% of 450 earners would leave a platform over delays [community survey, Trolley 2026].
9. Switched off or complained about: notifications (Splitwise, Emma, CRED), wrong auto-categorisation (Emma, Rocket Money), wrong "upcoming bill" detection (Rocket Money).
10. Late client payment is the freelancer's version of the same job; 85% report being paid late at least sometimes [secondary citation of Remote 2025], which is the case for a runway sentence plus a nudge action.

## UK and EU

**Monzo.** FY2025 report: 12m+ customers, 6.9m weekly active, 8.8m bill splits from 10.1m payment requests, 1m+ paying subscribers, 2.3m with Instant Access savings, 67% of customers join by word of mouth [vendor, 2025, https://monzo.com/annual-report/2025]. The report page gives no Pots or Salary Sorter figure. Salary Sorter works on any incoming payment over £100 and can save the split for next time [vendor, 2025, https://monzo.com/i/salary-sorter/]. Bills Pots arrived with Salary Sorter in 2019 [vendor, https://monzo.com/blog/2019/09/26/introducing-salary-sorter-and-bills-pots/]. About half of active customers use Monzo as their main bank (summary of the 2025 report, https://monzo.com/annual-report/2025). Reviews in the last week praise pots ("Pots are fab!", 2026), and one 5-star review of Aug 2026 warns the app is "less coherent" and that budgeting through pots was removed [community, 2026]. Transfer: the split-on-arrival habit has a UK paper trail; the Monzo US blog frames it as "make sure money for bills and rent is set aside" [vendor, 2025, https://monzo.com/blog/monzo-us-blog/auto-salary-sorter].

**Starling.** Spaces plus a bills manager that pays chosen direct debits and standing orders from a Space, with a notification when paid; it does not cover recurring card payments [vendor, 2021-2022, https://www.crowdfundinsider.com/2021/11/182481-uks-digital-bank-starling-introduces-bills-manager-to-help-consumers-better-manage-their-funds/]. No usage number published. Reviews: "the spaces to pay bills is very handy" and a request to see pending payments against balance [community, 2026]. [inference] The request is the runway job in one line.

**Revolut.** Subscriptions tracker (2020) warns before a scheduled payment or when funds are short [vendor, https://www.revolut.com/news/revolut_launches_smart_subscriptions_feature/]. Vault claim: over 1 million created [vendor, older, https://www.crowdfundinsider.com/?p=133247]. The newest reviews are almost all travel and currency exchange; none mentioned vaults, budgets or bills [community, 2026].

**Tricount, Splitwise, Lydia, bunq.** Tricount 2025 wrap: £20.2bn split globally; UK £1.16bn (+27.2%); busiest UK day 1 May with 11,282 tricounts created; average rent-and-utilities split £219.50; travel £234.39 [vendor, 2026, https://press.bunq.com/260369-shared-expenses-in-the-uk-hit-1-16-billion-as-social-spending-dominates]. In Ireland groceries are 26% of splits [vendor, 2026, https://press.bunq.com/260366-the-weekly-shop-becomes-ireland-s-most-split-expense-as-shared-spending-surges-55]. Tricount reviews are trips, couples, roommates; complaints are wrong maths and no CSV export. Splitwise reviews are trips and family; complaints are paywall and delay (10-second wait, daily entry limits) and notifications. Lydia is mainly a payment app with expense sharing as a module; no usage number found [vendor, https://www.clubic.com/application-mobile/guide-achat-479980-les-meilleures-applications-de-partage-de-frais-en-2023.html]. bunq passed 20m users [press, https://www.financemagnates.com/fintech/after-10-years-bunq-reaches-20-million-users-but-remains-far-behind-revolut].

**Emma, Snoop, Plum, Chip, N26.** Emma: reviews praise budget tracking and bank sync; complain about paywall, miscategorisation, notification volume and surprise annual charges [community, 2026]. A forum user said Emma and Snoop are the only aggregators they actually "use" [community, https://community.emma-app.com/t/snoop-discussion-feedback/2607]. Plum claimed round-ups lift savings by 50% [vendor, 2019, https://techcrunch.com/2019/11/13/plum-android/amp/, via summary]. No independent usage figures found for Snoop, Plum, Chip or N26.

**EU cross-cutting.** Decta survey (n=1,539 UK and EU, Apr-May 2025): 59.6% use several finance apps because a feature is missing; budgeting and spending analysis is the most missing feature (52%); 91% would consolidate into one app [vendor survey, 2025, https://fintechnews.ch/virtual-banking/european-consumers-favor-all-in-one-financial-app-format-but-missing-features-drive-multi-app-usage/76894/]. UK open banking: 13.3m active users, payments growing about 70% a year, used to pay credit card bills and fund savings [measured, OBL Impact Report 7, 2025, https://www.openbanking.org.uk/insights/obl-impact-report-7-open-banking-delivers-real-world-impact-as-adoption-accelerates-year-on-year/, summary].

## Americas

**Chime.** From the S-1 (to Mar 2025): SpotMe attach 49%, MyPay 26%, Credit Builder 37%, High Yield Savings 69%; $43.3bn accessed through SpotMe since 2019 and $8.8bn through MyPay since July 2024; 67% of 8.6m active members use Chime as primary; opens per member 141 a month; 85% of new direct-depositing members came from an existing direct-deposit relationship [measured, 2025, https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1795586/000162828025025059/chimefinancialinc-sx1wq1da.htm]. Round-ups and "Save When I Get Paid" are described but no adoption figure. Reviews cluster on two things: fee avoidance ("I was getting hit with 3 or 4 minimum overdraft charges every week", 2026) and early access to pay; the same week carries "The my pay is a loop hole scam" (2026) [community].

**Dave and Cash App.** Dave Q3 2025: 2.77m monthly transacting members, $2.0bn ExtraCash originations (+49%), average origination up 20%, 28-day delinquency 2.33% [measured, 2025, https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1841408/000119312525263535/d85365dex991.htm]. Reviews praise getting money until payday and complain about fees and pulls on a non-pay week ("started pulling money from my connected SEPARATE Bank account on my NON PAY WEEK", 2026) [community]. FTC-related reporting says more than 75% of new users never receive an advance, and the most common was $25 [community/press, 2025, https://www.salon.com/2025/01/17/cash-advance-apps-financial-relief-or-loan-shark-in-your-pocket]. Cash App reviews: sending to family, the card, and account closures.

**Rocket Money.** The sticky feature is finding and cancelling subscriptions; bill negotiation is the contested one (fee-on-savings, savings last a year, surprise charges). Upcoming-bills detection is a mixed bag in the newest reviews: "illustrates all expenses and bills 2x" and "shows my car payment 50x in a single month" (2026) next to "tells you when bills are coming up" (2024) [community]. Forced trial and hard-to-cancel complaints dominate the last week.

**Qapital, round-ups.** Reviews like the "round up rule" and "set it and forget it", but trust breaks fast when an automatic transfer misfires ($150 missing, 2026) [community]. No independent evidence on round-up outcomes was found; Plum's 50% is a vendor claim.

**Nubank and Mercado Pago.** Nubank: 131m customers and 83% activity at end 2025 (summary); in Brazil the app is the card bill (fatura), Pix, Caixinhas and loans. Automated Pix (June 2025) searches upcoming bills and lets the user approve or decline each one [vendor, 2025, https://nu.com/en/newsroom/consumers/nubank-launches-automated-pix-with-smart-bill-search]. Latest reviews are about card limit, loans, Caixinhas security and yield; none this week mention boleto [community, 2026]. Mercado Pago: 72m monthly users; credit card the standout product [measured, 2025]. [inference] Pix scheduling and the "approve each bill" model of automated Pix is the closest shipped analogue to the Approve button.

**Canada.** KOHO and Wealthsimple: customer counts only; the feature split was not found.

## India and South-East Asia

**UPI AutoPay.** Jan 2025: 175m AutoPay transactions (58m in Jan 2024), 35m mandates created (14.5m), 53% of recurring payments (33% a year earlier); cards fell from 42% to 31% [measured, NPCI via Moneycontrol, quoted by Equentis, 2025, https://www.equentis.com/blog/?p=52612]. July 2025 mandate registrations reached about 50m (summary, https://yourstory.com/2025/09/upi-crosses-20-billion-monthly-payments-for-the-first-time). Revocations on low customer balance hit 20m a month [press headline, 2025, https://www.business-standard.com/amp/finance/news/upi-autopay-revocations-hit-20-mn-monthly-over-low-customer-balances-125090700500_1.html; page returned 403, headline only]. NPCI moved AutoPay executions to non-peak hours from 1 Aug 2025 (summary). [inference] The mandate is a calendar of debits; the failure is cash arriving late.

**CRED.** Core job: pay and track credit card bills. Newest reviews split between that ("the only reason i use cred is to pay and track my credit card bills") and anger about rewards and bill-due spam ("still spamming bill due messages.", 2026) [community]. A 2021 share of about 20% of card-bill payments was quoted (summary, weak).

**Fi, Jupiter, Walnut/Axio.** Fi served more than 3.5m customers and shut its banking in March 2026 as it pivoted to AI [measured, press, https://techcrunch.com/2026/03/11/india-neobank-fi-winds-down-banking-services-on-its-platform]. Jupiter pulled back from an acquisition to focus on profitability (summary). SMS-reading trackers get installs (about 14.5m for Axio, estimate) but carry a privacy objection [community]. [inference] Insights apps did not win a standalone business; payment rails and cards did.

**Philippines.** GCash: bill payments to 1,500+ billers; the iOS rating is 3.04 and recent reviews concern failed bill payments and transfers [vendor + community, 2026]. Payoneer/GCash study (2022): overseas-client freelancers use Payoneer for foreign money and GCash for local; funds withdraw to PHP instantly and free in GCash [vendor, https://www.sunstar.com.ph/manila/business/study-filipino-freelancers-with-overseas-clients-on-the-rise]. This is the closest match to Ben: foreign-currency income, local-currency life.

**Korea.** Toss: send money is the entry habit; 30m registered by Jul 2025; reviews praise the locked savings account and ask for more remittance countries [community, 2026]. The share of transactions that are sends was not found.

**China.** Alipay and WeChat Pay: only old figures found (Yu'e Bao 149m users in 2014); no current bill-pay breakdown [weak, https://www.caixinglobal.com/2013-06-20/alipays-investment-service-attracts-1-mln-users-in-week-101014322.html].

## Africa and MENA

**M-Pesa and Fuliza.** 40m monthly users; KES 38.3tn transacted in FY to Mar 2025; M-Pesa is 41.5% of Safaricom revenue [measured, 2026, https://www.the-star.co.ke/news/realtime/2026-03-06-m-pesa-hits-40-million-users-on-19th-birthday]. Fuliza (half-year to Sep 2025): 9.1m customers (+22.2%), KES 629.2bn disbursed (+39.8%), 23.5% of the M-Pesa base [measured, Safaricom via press, 2025, https://techweez.com/2025/11/06/fuliza-revenue-growth/]. A CBK study put adult uptake at 18.3% (summary). Fuliza covers shortfalls at bill payments, till payments and sending money [press, https://www.standardmedia.co.ke/article/2001456831/fuliza-economy-kenyans-borrow-sh16b-daily-amid-economic-woes]. No category split by spend was found.

**OPay and PalmPay.** OPay 39.3m monthly users in FY2025, PalmPay 15m daily transactions in Q1 2025 (summary) [vendor]. Both sell airtime, data, electricity and TV; PalmPay cashback draws a subset of users [community]. A Nigerian habits survey put airtime and data as the second-largest expense at 46.1% (summary; Column, 2025).

**Tabby and Tamara.** 77% of 2,500 surveyed Tabby shoppers planned to use BNPL for essentials such as education, groceries and medical in 2024; Tabby and Tamara reported SAR 535.4m combined revenue in Q1 2025 (summary) [vendor, https://tabby.ai/en-AE/newsroom/essential-spending].

## Cross-border earners

**Payoneer, Wise, Deel, Grey, Geegpay.** Wise: 15.6m active customers, £145.2bn volume FY2025 [measured]; reviews are overwhelmingly "send money abroad quickly" and none mentioned freelancing or a USD account [community, 2026]. Grey/Geegpay: virtual USD account, convert when the rate suits, withdraw locally; fees 0.8% (Grey) [vendor]. Trolley 2026 (n=450, 19 countries, May-Jun 2026): 82% want a say in when they are paid, 73% get whatever the platform sets, 72% treat two days as the outer wait, 93% would take less for a 24-hour guarantee, 41% have left or would leave a platform over payment delays [community survey, https://trolley.com/resources/recipient-report-2026/]. Late payment: 85% of freelancers were paid late at least sometimes, 21% more than half the time; 63% wait 30 days or more (Remote 2025 and another survey, both via a secondary page) [secondary, https://satchel.eu/blog/the-freelancers-guide-to-getting-paid-faster]. Remitly reviews value real-time tracking of a transfer [community, 2026].

## Automation findings

- **Autopay adoption (US):** 41% use autopay; 48.2% over $100k income vs 31% under $50k; paycheck-to-paycheck consumers 26% for most bills; streaming 63% vs rent 22% [vendor survey, PYMNTS, Jan 2025, https://www.pymnts.com/?p=2432924]. KUBRA: 23% all bills, 59% some, 18% none; non-users prefer to review bills first (67%) and control timing (58%); 74% of users pay by credit card [vendor survey, May 2025, https://www.kubra.com/blog/autopay-and-bill-payments--whos-in-and-whos-opting-out].
- **Autopay (India):** growth is fast (53% of recurring payments), but low-balance revocations run at 20m a month [measured/press, 2025, links above].
- **Early salary access:** Chime MyPay is used monthly by 26% of active members; SpotMe by 49% [measured, S-1, 2025]. Dave and Fuliza show the same pattern with larger loans. Hospitality EWA study (n about 70) found bill paying and food top concerns [community, EBRI 2025, https://www.ebri.org/content/new-research-report-surveying-hospitality-industry-workers-utilizing-earned-wage-access-tech-finds-bill-paying--food-and-money-availability-top-concerns].
- **Salary sorting on payday:** Monzo Salary Sorter, Qapital "payday divy" and rules, Toss locked savings; no adoption numbers published for Monzo or Qapital. Reviewers like set-it-and-forget-it and punish glitches.
- **Round-ups:** liked in reviews; no independent outcome data [vendor claim only, Plum 2019].
- **What people switch off or leave:** notifications (Splitwise "Deleted it just to stop the useless notifications"; Emma; CRED bill-due spam), wrong auto-categorisation (Emma, Rocket Money), manual entry and shame framing (blog-level evidence only: Apptopia 30-day retention of 38% for finance apps was quoted by unverified pages) [community/weak, 2025-2026, https://wealthwiseos.pages.dev/blog/why-budgeting-apps-fail-30-days].
- **Gaps (not found):** Pots, Salary Sorter, Spaces, Caixinhas adoption rates; Snoop, Plum, Chip, N26, Lydia usage; PhonePe/Paytm bill-pay share; Alipay bill pay; Walnut/Axio official users; Toss sends share; M-Shwari figures; Reddit threads.

## The jobs that won everywhere

Ranked by (evidence of heavy use) x (fit to a 30-day runway for PayPal earners).

1. **Warn me before a known debit hits an empty account.** Where it won: UK (Starling Bills Manager, Monzo bills pots, Revolut Subscriptions alert), India (UPI AutoPay mandates, CRED reminders), US (Rocket Money upcoming bills), Brazil (Nubank automated Pix with approval). Evidence: 175m AutoPay transactions a month in India yet 20m revocations a month for low balance; US paycheck-to-paycheck users use autopay half as much [measured/vendor, 2025]. Transfers directly: the sentence "if Acme is late you are ¥18,780 short on the 27th" is this job with income uncertainty added. [inference]
2. **Bridge the days before the next payment.** Where it won: US (Chime SpotMe 49% attach, MyPay 26%; Dave $2.0bn a quarter), Kenya (Fuliza 9.1m customers). Why it transfers: it shows the real cause, a timing gap. For us the safe version is information (how short, on which day) plus the PayPal action that closes the gap by chasing the client, not a credit product. [measured, 2025]
3. **See that a payment I am owed or sent has arrived.** Where it won: remittance (Remitly tracking, Wise speed), Korea (Toss send), Kenya (M-Pesa), Brazil (Pix), Philippines (GCash). Trolley: 72% accept at most two days; 41% would leave a platform over delay. Transfers: invoice paid / not paid is the state the sentence changes on. [community survey, 2026]
4. **Nudge a late payer.** Where it won: freelancer tools generally; evidence is demand not product: 85% paid late at least sometimes (secondary). Transfers: it is the single Approve action. [secondary, 2025]
5. **Set money aside the moment income lands.** Where it won: UK (Monzo Salary Sorter and bills pots), US (Qapital payday divy), Korea (Toss locked savings), Brazil (Caixinhas). Why it transfers: earners get lumpy income, so "reserve for rent and bills first" suits a runway number; the rule is trusted only when it does not misfire (Qapital $150 report, 2026). [vendor+community]
6. **Split a shared cost and settle up.** Where it won: Europe (Tricount £20.2bn, Monzo 8.8m splits), US (Splitwise, Venmo/Cash App for friends), Canada via e-transfer (not measured). Rent and utilities average £219.50 per split in the UK. Transfers: shared rent is a fixed event on the 30-day timeline. People delete the app over notifications and paywalls, so keep it quiet. [vendor, 2026]
7. **Find and cancel subscriptions.** Where it won: US (Rocket Money, 404,611 ratings), UK (Revolut, Emma, Monzo), Europe. Evidence is anecdotal; detection errors are the top complaint. Transfers: subscriptions are small dated outflows on the timeline; show them, don't auto-cancel. [community, 2026]
8. **Pay the card bill in full on time.** Where it won: India (CRED), Brazil (Nubank fatura, Mercado Pago card). A card statement date is the biggest dated debit for many users; the unbilled card spend line in our product is this job. [community+measured]
9. **Hold foreign currency, convert on my terms, withdraw locally.** Where it won: Wise (15.6m customers), Payoneer-into-GCash, Grey/Geegpay for African freelancers, Revolut for travel. Ben is paid in USD and lives in yen, so the question "what is this client's USD worth in JPY on the 27th" is core. [measured+vendor]
10. **Top up and pay everyday bills from one wallet.** Where it won: Nigeria (OPay, PalmPay airtime/data/electricity), Philippines (GCash 1,500 billers), India (PhonePe, Paytm), Kenya (M-Pesa). Fixed small recurring outflows; low value for a runway sentence beyond listing them. [vendor]
11. **One app for everything.** Where it won: Toss, Alipay/WeChat, Nubank. Decta 2025: 91% would consolidate; missing budgeting is the top gap (52%). Transfers as an argument for keeping the product to one screen. [vendor survey, 2025]
12. **Round-ups.** Where it won: US (Qapital) and UK (Plum, Chip) with loyal niche fans, no independent outcome data. Low priority for earners with irregular income. [vendor]

Did not win anywhere I could find: unprompted budgets with manual categorising, bill negotiation as a headline job (liked when it works, resented when fees surprise), and insight-only dashboards (Fi wound down; Emma reviews dominated by paywall and miscategorisation complaints). [inference from the evidence above]
