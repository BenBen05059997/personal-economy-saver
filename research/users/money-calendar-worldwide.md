# The money calendar worldwide: when people do money things, and what they ask

Researched 3 Nov 2026. Web research only; about 40 searches plus one page fetch. Tags: [measured] survey, statute or dataset; [community] forum post; [vendor] company claim; [inference] my reading. Quotes are under 15 words.

## Summary (10 lines)

1. Pay is the clock. Five patterns cover the 14 countries: monthly at month-end (UK, DE, FR, JP, NG, KR, ID), the 5th business day (BR), semi-monthly 15th/30th (PH, MX), biweekly or weekly (US, CA, AU), and a 25th-ish payday (JP, ID, KR, BR in practice).
2. Thirteenth-month style pay is law or custom in many places: Brazil (Nov 30 and Dec 20), Philippines (by Dec 24), Mexico aguinaldo (by Dec 20), Indonesia THR (7 days before the holiday), Japan and Korea bonuses. They land in Nov-Dec and are spent before the January hangover.
3. The big spending peaks are fixed-date: Black Friday and Cyber Monday (US, UK, CA, BR), 11.11 (China-linked), Buen Fin (MX, Nov 13-17 in 2025), Diwali (IN), Ramadan/Eid (ID, NG), Lunar New Year (KR and Asia), back-to-school (US, July-Aug).
4. Rent is due at the start of the period almost everywhere (DE statute: third working day; US and CA by convention [inference]); Australia pays weekly or fortnightly, so rent follows the pay cycle.
5. Card bills are a second clock: US law requires the statement to arrive at least 21 days before the due date, so the "bill that is coming" is knowable weeks ahead.
6. Renewal moments are dated: DE car insurance cancel by 30 Nov, DE health fund Zusatzbeitrag change in Jan, US Medicare 15 Oct-7 Dec, US ACA from 1 Nov, UK energy cap quarterly, UK subscription rules tightening.
7. Government payments hitting the self-employed are dated and recurring (US 15 Apr/Jun/Sep/Jan; UK 31 Jan/31 Jul; DE 10 Mar/Jun/Sep/Dec; IN 15 Jun/Sep/Dec/Mar; AU 28 Oct/Feb/Apr/Jul; CA 15 Mar/Jun/Sep/Dec; BR MEI DAS on the 20th; ES autónomo last business day; FR URSSAF 30 Apr/31 Jul/31 Oct/31 Jan).
8. Evidence gap: I could not get Google Trends data directly (the tool cannot fetch it). The measured seasonality I found is thin: UK "holidays" peaks in January; "pay rent" peaks on the 1st is only a marketing-blog claim.
9. Day-of-month spending evidence is mostly vendor surveys; no central-bank or statistics-office day-of-month series turned up (the Bank of England card series was discontinued in 2025).
10. For a 30-day runway app, the shared, non-tax chores are: pay-cycle gap forecasting, rent/bill day alignment, year-end bonus and festival envelopes, subscription sweeps, renewal cancel-by dates, card due-date pairing and late-client invoice timing.

## 1. Pay cycles and "last week before payday"

- US: pay is mostly biweekly or weekly. Of private establishments, 43.0% paid every two weeks and 27.0% weekly (Feb 2023); this counts establishments, not employees. [measured] [BLS CES](https://www.bls.gov/ces/publications/length-pay-period.htm), 2023.
- Brazil: salary is due by the 5th business day of the following month; bank guidance moves a Saturday payment to Friday. 13th salary: first instalment by 30 Nov, second by 20 Dec. [measured/legal summary] [DCI](https://www.dci.com.br/economia/que-dia-cai-o-quinto-dia-util-de-dezembro-de-2024/316609), [Workmotion](https://workmotion-bi2a4.stonly.com/kb/guide/en/brazil-workmotion-brazil-13th-salary-calculations-rules-and-payment-deadlines-eLOkEzKOs1/Steps/5404469), 2024-25. Caveat: sources disagree on whether Saturday counts as a business day.
- Philippines: the Labor Code requires pay at least twice a month, intervals not over 16 days; most firms pay on the 15th and month-end, and a weekend payday moves to the prior business day. [vendor/legal commentary] [Respicio](https://www.respicio.ph/commentaries/how-often-should-wages-be-paid-philippines-labor-code), 2025. 13th-month pay: by 24 Dec every year (PD 851), optionally half in May/June. [measured] [Inquirer on DOLE](https://newsinfo.inquirer.net/2017988/dole-reminds-employers-give-13th-month-pay-before-christmas/amp), 2023-24.
- Mexico: aguinaldo is at least 15 days of salary, due by 20 Dec (Federal Labor Law art. 87). [legal summary] [Teamed glossary](https://www.teamed.global/glossary/aguinaldo), 2025. That quincena (15th/30th) payroll rhythm is [inference]; I did not find a primary source for it.
- Indonesia: the most common private payday is the 25th, with a typical range of 23rd-27th; BUMN (state firms) usually the 25th. [vendor] [KantorKu](https://kantorku.id/blog/gajian-tanggal-berapa/), 2025. THR: due no later than 7 days before the holiday, 5% fine if late. [legal news] [Hukumonline](https://prov1.hukumonline.com/a/lt65fa03c038dcc/late-thr-payments-to-employees-to-result-in-fines-of-up-to-5-of-total-thr/), 2024.
- Nigeria: monthly, usually by the last working day; a 2014 health-facility survey found officially due days from the 21st to the 31st. [vendor + dated survey] [Pebl](https://help.hellopebl.com/en-US/pebl/article/o20wD4Js-salary-payments-in-nigeria), [IHSN](https://catalog.ihsn.org/catalog/6691/variable/F45/V2243?name=Q407). Media describe workers broke before payday ([News Express, Oct 2025](https://newsexpressngr.com/news/279298/why-nigerians-go-broke-before-payday)) but with no data. [community-grade]
- Korea: bonuses at Chuseok and Lunar New Year; 2026 KFSME survey: 45.1% of SMEs plan Chuseok bonuses, average about 665,000 won among fixed-amount payers. [measured] [Korea Times, Sept 2026](https://www.koreatimes.co.kr/economy/20260919/more-than-half-of-smes-to-skip-chuseok-bonuses). Payday on the 25th: not verified in my searches. [inference]
- Japan (brief): Keidanren summer bonus 2025 final tally about ¥974,000 weighted average; the 2026 first tally topped ¥1 million. [measured] [Japan Times, 2025](https://www.japantimes.co.jp/business/2025/09/14/companies/major-japan-firms-summer-bonuses-record), [nippon.com, 2026](https://www.nippon.com/en/news/yjj2026080600806/).
- UK, Germany, France, Canada, Australia payday dates: not sourced here; treat "monthly in UK/DE/FR, biweekly in AU/CA" as [inference].
- Pre-payday behaviour: a military payday-loan working paper found food spending spikes on payday and is lower at period end [measured, working paper, found via search]; IZA DP 1430 finds cash spending declines over the pay period while card spending stays flat [measured] ([IZA](https://www.iza.org/index.php/publications/dp1430/1/citations/endnote)). EarnIn/Talker Research: nearly 48% of a paycheck spent within 48 hours [vendor] ([Scripps](https://www.scrippsnews.com/life/money/americans-spend-over-a-third-of-their-paycheck-within-12-hours-study-shows)). UK Post Office/CEBR: people spend £55 on payday versus £26 daily average [vendor] ([Post Office](https://www.mynewsdesk.com/uk/post-office/pressreleases/payday-splurge-brits-double-their-daily-spending-on-payday-post-office-and-cebr-reveal-uk-adults-unconsciously-spend-more-than-900-pounds-of-their-dot-dot-dot-2874036)).

## 2. Rent, mortgage, utilities, card statements

- Germany: rent for housing is due at the start of the period, at the latest the third working day; Saturday is not a working day (BGH 2010). [measured/legal] [lxgesetze BGB 556b](https://lxgesetze.de/bgb/556b), [rewis BGH](https://rewis.io/urteile/urteil/lfp-13-07-2010-viii-zr-29109/).
- Australia: rent is quoted weekly but paid weekly, fortnightly or monthly by state; Victoria over 70% monthly, Queensland mostly weekly/fortnightly. [vendor/industry] [Urban.com.au](https://www.urban.com.au/expert-insights/investing/19260-massive-differences-in-rent-frequency-between-qld-and-vic).
- US, UK, CA, other countries' most common rent day: no measured source found. "1st of the month" is [inference]. A marketing blog claims "pay rent" searches peak on the 1st [vendor, unverified].
- Canada (Ontario): rent increase needs 90 days' written notice, once per 12 months; 2026 guideline 2.1%. [measured/legal] [ontario.ca](https://ontario.ca/page/residential-rent-increases).
- US cards: statements must be mailed or delivered at least 21 days before the due date. [legal] [helpwithmybank.gov](https://helpwithmybank.gov/help-topics/credit-cards/payments/making-payments/making-payments-statement.html), 2010 rules.
- France: taxe foncière 2026 due 15 Oct (non-online) or 20 Oct (online); 2027 monthly option must be requested before 15 Dec 2026. [measured] [MoneyVox, 2026](https://www.moneyvox.fr/impot/actualites/110477/taxe-fonciere-2026-date-limite-le-15-octobre-pour-le-paiement-a-la-dgfip). Treat as a dated household bill, not self-employed tax.
- UK energy: Ofgem price cap changes quarterly (1 Jan, 1 Apr, 1 Jul, 1 Oct), announced weeks ahead. [measured] [Ofgem](https://www.ofgem.gov.uk/publications/ofgem-announces-latest-quarterly-price-cap-update), 2022-25. Energy UK data shows January switching is mixed: sometimes a slow month, sometimes up ([Energy Live News, Feb 2024](https://www.energylivenews.com/2024/02/19/energy-supplier-switching-soars-year-on-year/)).
- Mortgage day, Japanese direct-debit days, Korean card pull dates: not researched. [gap]

## What this means for a 30-day runway app

Non-tax chores the app could take off the user, ranked by how many of the 14 countries share them. Country counts are my reading of the sections above.

1. Pay-cycle gap forecast: "your balance on the day before payday". All 14 countries; the pay pattern differs (monthly, biweekly, semi-monthly, 5th business day) but the question is the same. For freelancers on PayPal the "payday" is the client's payment run, not an employer's.
2. Rent and fixed-bill day alignment: show rent, utilities and mortgage on the same timeline as income, and warn when a bill lands before payday (DE third working day, AU fortnightly rent). About 14 countries; the statute or custom differs.
3. Late-client chase timed to the client's pay run: nudge before month-end, before the 15th/30th (PH, MX) or the 25th (ID, JP) run, and before 5th-business-day cutoffs (BR). About 14 countries, and a direct fit for the PayPal "nudge a late client" action.
4. Festival and gift envelope: a set-aside that starts 6-8 weeks before Christmas, Diwali, Eid, Lunar New Year, Buen Fin or Black Friday, with a "what January looks like" number. About 12 countries (Christmas in US/UK/DE/FR/BR/MX/PH/CA/AU/NG, Diwali IN, Eid ID/NG, Lunar New Year KR).
5. January hangover forecast: show the card statement and subscription bills that will land in January after December spending. US, UK, CA, AU, DE, FR and any country with Christmas spending, about 10 countries.
6. Card due-date pairing: show the statement balance, the due date, and whether the payday comes first; where the law guarantees 21 days between statement and due date (US), the figure is knowable weeks ahead. About 8 countries (US, CA, UK, AU, JP, KR, BR, MX) [inference on non-US cards].
7. Subscription and auto-renewal sweep: list recurring charges, flag annual renewals within 30 days, and let the user cancel or snooze. About 9 countries (UK evidence is strongest; UK rules tighten from 2027).
8. Year-end bonus planner: when a 13th-month, aguinaldo, THR or bonus is due (BR 30 Nov/20 Dec, PH 24 Dec, MX 20 Dec, ID 7 days before Eid, JP Jun/Dec, KR Chuseok/Lunar New Year), allocate it before Black Friday, Buen Fin and Eid-travel spending. 6 to 7 countries; for the freelancer, the equivalent is a self-made "13th month".
9. Renewal cancel-by dates: car and home insurance (DE 30 Nov, UK auto-renew), health plan windows (US 15 Oct-7 Dec Medicare, ACA from 1 Nov; DE Zusatzbeitrag in January), energy (UK quarterly cap). About 5 to 6 countries with fixed windows; the rest are anniversary-based.
10. Rent-increase and lease-renewal notice: warn 90 days ahead (Ontario) and show the new monthly total on the runway. About 4 to 5 countries (CA, US, UK, DE, AU); evidence is regulatory, not behavioural.
11. Set-aside for recurring government payments (tax-adjacent, but not tax advice): a "next dated payment" line for estimated tax, MEI DAS (20th), URSSAF, autónomo fee, PAYG. 9 countries have a dated schedule (see section 6); the user asked for non-tax, so I rank this last and keep it to a date and an amount to set aside.
12. Shopping-event guardrail: before Black Friday, 11.11, Boxing Day or Buen Fin, show "safe to spend" after upcoming bills. About 6 countries (US, UK, CA, BR, MX, plus China-linked 11.11 markets).

Caveats: pay-day and rent-day distributions by country are mostly vendor or statute-level; no measured day-of-month distribution was found for any country except US pay frequency (BLS, 2023). Several country gaps are flagged in-line as [gap].
