# Wealth-moves research: row template

Every move in every regional file uses this shape, so the rows can later become the app's knowledge catalogue (one JSON file per region). Fill every field; write "unknown" rather than guessing.

```
### <Move name, in English; local name in parentheses>

- **Group:** quick money | tax | invest & cash | insurance & benefits | get paid better | stop the leak | unclaimed money
- **Who it is for:** eligibility in plain words (residency, income band, employment type, age...)
- **What it is:** 2–4 sentences a 22-year-old would understand
- **Value:** the formula in words, then one worked example with the local currency figure. Say which inputs it needs (income, tax bracket, balance, months...).
- **Time to money:** days / weeks / months / years
- **Risk:** none | low | market | behavioural (explain in one line)
- **Effort:** minutes | an evening | a weekend | ongoing
- **Deadline or season:** e.g. Dec 31, tax-year end, enrolment lag
- **Traps and caveats:** what goes wrong, what people get refused for, hidden fees, the version of this that is a scam
- **Why people miss it:** one or two lines
- **What we may say:** information we can show (with the user's numbers) vs wording that becomes regulated advice or intermediation in this jurisdiction
- **Freelancer paid from abroad:** anything that changes for someone invoiced in a foreign currency via PayPal (or "nothing")
- **Sources:** primary first (government, regulator, the institution's own page), each with the URL and the date checked (Oct 31, 2026 unless noted)
- **Confidence:** verified primary | secondary only | anecdotal (say which figures are which)
```

Rules for the research:
- Primary sources over blogs. Japanese and other local-language official pages are fine; quote the figure and translate.
- Affiliate blogs are never the only source for a number. If only blogs have it, say so and mark anecdotal.
- Give 2026 figures where rules changed recently; note the change and its date.
- Do not invent numbers. "unknown, verify" is a valid value.
- Include the moves people underestimate most, the wealth destroyers (what quietly drains money), and unclaimed money (benefits, refunds, rebates, dormant accounts). Think past the obvious.
