# Japan: tax and freelancer moves (checked Oct 31, 2026)

## Summary: top five for a Tokyo freelancer earning about ¥6M a year in USD
Worked-example profile used below: Tokyo (Setagaya FY2026 国保 rates), under 40, single. Revenue ¥6.0M, expenses ¥1.0M, 青色 ¥650k, so 事業所得 is ¥4.35M. Social insurance is about ¥0.70M, 所得税 taxable income about ¥2.61M (10% band), and 住民税所得割 about ¥320k.
Marginal cost of each extra ¥1 of profit: 所得税 10.21% + 住民税 10% + 国保 10.58% + 個人事業税 5% ≈ **36%**. Each ¥1 of 所得控除 (iDeCo, 共済) saves only ≈ **20%**, because it does not cut 国保 or 事業税.
1. **青色申告 with e-Tax (¥650k) instead of white or ¥100k:** about **¥170k–200k a year** (¥550k–650k × ~31%). Deadline: 承認申請 by Mar 15 of the year. From 2027, ¥750k with 優良電子帳簿; paper filing drops to ¥100k.
2. **iDeCo (¥68k a month, ¥75k from Dec 2026) plus 小規模企業共済 (up to ¥70k a month):** about **¥157k a year** with iDeCo alone at the cap, about **¥284k** with both at the cap. The money is locked until 60 (iDeCo) or until you close the business.
3. **Claim every real expense, including the business share of rent and utilities (家事按分):** about **¥36k saved per ¥100k** of expenses you were missing. Under the 2026 rules each ¥1 of expense is also worth more, because of the next item.
4. **Stay at or below ¥4.89M 合計所得 in 2026–27:** the 所得税 basic deduction jumps from ¥670k to ¥1.04M at that line, worth **¥38k–76k**. Only expenses and 青色控除 lower 合計所得; iDeCo and 共済 do not.
5. **Elect to be a consumption-tax payer (課税事業者選択) as a mostly-export freelancer:** your sales to US clients are 0%-rated, so you get back the 10% consumption tax on your Japanese expenses. That is about **¥100k a year per ¥1.1M** of taxable spending, minus bookkeeping effort. You must file by **Dec 31, 2026** to cover 2027, and you are then locked in for 2 years.
Deadlines right now: 予定納税 減額 Nov 1–15, 2026. 課税事業者選択届出書 and ふるさと納税/expense purchases by Dec 31, 2026. 優良電子帳簿 set up from Jan 1, 2027 for the ¥750k 青色. 青色承認 by Mar 15, 2027.
Not verified (marked in rows): tax card-payment fee, セルフメディケーション extension after 2026, 法人成り breakeven, 経営セーフティ ¥8M cap, white-filer 家事按分 50% rule.
Smaller items: 国民年金 2-year prepayment (¥17,370), ふるさと納税 (about ¥80k donation limit, about ¥22k net in goods), 予定納税 reduction request (deadline **Nov 15, 2026**), 更正の請求 for up to 5 past years.

### iDeCo for the self-employed (個人型確定拠出年金, 第1号被保険者)

- **Group:** tax
- **Who it is for:** Anyone paying 国民年金 as a 第1号被保険者 (self-employed, freelancers, students over 20). You must actually be paying 国民年金. If you are on a 免除 (exemption) you generally cannot contribute; verify for your case. From Dec 2026 you can contribute up to age 70 (rules apply).
- **What it is:** A private pension account where every yen you put in is deducted from your taxable income, the investments grow tax-free, and you get the money from age 60. It is the single biggest deduction most freelancers can use.
- **Value:** contribution × (所得税 marginal rate × 1.021 + 住民税 10%). Inputs: monthly contribution and taxable income band. Cap for 第1号: **¥68,000 a month through Nov 2026, ¥75,000 a month from the Dec 2026 contribution (debited Jan 2027)**. Both caps are combined with 国民年金基金 and 付加保険料. With 付加年金 the cap is ¥67,000 now and ¥74,000 after the change. Example: ¥68,000 × 12 = ¥816k. In the profile, 所得税 falls about ¥75k (part of it drops into the 5% band) and 住民税 falls about ¥82k, so the total is about **¥157k a year**. Under the new cap, ¥900k a year would save about ¥170k.
- **Time to money:** The tax saving comes at the next 確定申告 and the following 住民税. The money itself is locked until 60.
- **Risk:** market (the funds you pick can fall; deposit products exist) and behavioural (the lock-up)
- **Effort:** an evening to open; then ongoing (automatic debit)
- **Deadline or season:** Contributions count in the year they are debited. Dec contributions are debited in January, so they count in the next year. To raise the amount for Dec 2026, providers asked for change forms by end of Oct 2026. If you missed that, file a normal change.
- **Traps and caveats:** Small monthly account fees continue even when you stop contributing (amount: unknown, verify with your provider). The deduction does **not** lower 国保 premiums, 個人事業税 or 合計所得 (so it does not help with the ¥4.89M basic-deduction line). Withdrawals are taxed as 退職所得 or 公的年金等 when you take them. Missing 国民年金 payments can make you ineligible. Deposit-only choices give the tax saving with no growth.
- **Why people miss it:** They think it is a company-pension thing. Freelancers have the highest cap of anyone, but nobody auto-enrols them.
- **What we may say:** We may show the cap, the deduction and a tax-saving estimate from the user's numbers. Recommending specific funds or a provider is regulated activity (DC運営管理 / 投資助言), so keep to general information.
- **Freelancer paid from abroad:** Nothing changes. USD income is converted to yen on the return. Contributions are in yen from a Japanese bank account.
- **Sources:** MHLW leaflet "iDeCoがパワーアップします！令和8年12月から": 第1号 ¥68,000 → ¥75,000, "国民年金基金と合わせて75,000円が上限" https://www.mhlw.go.jp/content/12500000/001620594.pdf ; NTA No.1135 (full deduction) https://www.nta.go.jp/taxes/shiraberu/taxanswer/shotoku/1135.htm ; MUFG iDeCo notice (¥67,000 with 付加 now, ¥74,000 after, debited Jan 2027, form by end Oct 2026) https://www.bk.mufg.jp/tameru/ideco/info/pdf/pop_20260813_02.pdf ; Resona FAQ https://www.resonabank.co.jp/nenkin/ideco/qa/faq8540.html (all checked Oct 31, 2026)
- **Confidence:** Caps and the Dec 2026 date are verified primary (MHLW). The ¥67k/¥74k figures with 付加 come from provider documents (secondary). Fee amounts: unknown, verify.

### Small Business Mutual Aid (小規模企業共済)

- **Group:** tax
- **Who it is for:** Sole proprietors (個人事業主) and directors of small companies, and some co-managers. Freelancers who filed a 開業届 and run a business qualify. Eligibility check is done by 中小機構.
- **What it is:** A government "retirement pay for the self-employed" fund. You pay ¥1,000–¥70,000 a month, and all of it is deducted from taxable income. When you close the business or retire you get it back, taxed lightly as retirement income. You can also borrow against it cheaply.
- **Value:** contribution × (所得税 marginal × 1.021 + 10%). Max ¥840k a year. Up to 1 year of prepaid contributions is also deductible in the year paid. Example: ¥840k on its own in the profile saves about **¥160k a year**. Stacked with a maxed iDeCo, the total for both is about ¥284k, because the second one falls in the 5% band. Loans against your balance: **general 1.5% a year, special 0.9% a year** (unpaid loans incur 14.6% late interest).
- **Time to money:** The tax saving comes the following spring. The payout comes when you close or retire (or you can borrow sooner).
- **Risk:** low (guaranteed scheme). Behavioural: cancelling early loses money.
- **Effort:** an evening (apply through a bank or 商工会)
- **Deadline or season:** Contributions count in the year paid. The Dec 31 prepayment trick lets you deduct up to 12 months ahead in the current year.
- **Traps and caveats:** If you cancel within 12 months you get **nothing back**. If you cancel voluntarily before 240 months (20 years) you get less than you paid in, and voluntary cancellation under 65 is taxed as 一時所得. Like iDeCo, it does not lower 国保 or 事業税. If you miss payments for 12 months it can be cancelled. Lower the contribution rather than cancel when cash is tight.
- **Why people miss it:** The name sounds like it is for "small companies", not one-person freelancers, and it is sold through 商工会 and banks, not apps.
- **What we may say:** We may show the deduction maths and the cancellation table. Telling someone exactly how much to contribute based on their situation drifts toward 税理士 or FP advice; keep it as "here is what ¥X saves".
- **Freelancer paid from abroad:** Nothing changes. Contributions are in yen. Foreign-client income is still 事業所得, so you qualify.
- **Sources:** 中小機構 掛金 page ("月額1,000円～最高70,000円の範囲内（500円単位）", "全額を小規模企業共済等掛金控除", "1年以内の前納掛金も同様に控除") https://kyosai-web.smrj.go.jp/customer/skyosai/installment/ ; 中小機構 drawbacks page (12-month and 240-month rules, tax treatment) https://skyosai.smrj.go.jp/skyosai-demerit/ ; FAQ on loan rates ("一般貸付けの利率は年利1.5%、特別貸付けの利率は年利0.9％") https://kyosai-faq.smrj.go.jp/skyosai/index.php?action=faq&cat=37&id=190&artlang=ja ; NTA No.1135 https://www.nta.go.jp/taxes/shiraberu/taxanswer/shotoku/1135.htm
- **Confidence:** verified primary for the contribution range, deduction, 12/240-month rules and loan rates (the loan FAQ shows no date). The payout yield/予定利率 is unknown, verify.

### National Pension Fund (国民年金基金)

- **Group:** insurance & benefits
- **Who it is for:** 第1号被保険者 paying full 国民年金 (not exempt), roughly ages 20–65.
- **What it is:** A top-up public-style pension for the self-employed. You choose a guaranteed lifelong annuity plan, and the premiums are fully deductible as 社会保険料控除. It shares one cap with iDeCo.
- **Value:** Tax saving uses the same formula as iDeCo. The combined cap with iDeCo is ¥68,000 a month, rising to ¥75,000 from Dec 2026. The benefit is a guaranteed annuity instead of a market fund. Example: ¥20,000 a month = ¥240k a year deduction, about ¥48k a year saved in the profile.
- **Time to money:** The tax saving is yearly. The pension starts at 65.
- **Risk:** low (fixed promise). Behavioural: it is hard to stop or withdraw, and there is no lump sum when you leave early.
- **Effort:** an evening
- **Deadline or season:** any time. Deductible in the year paid.
- **Traps and caveats:** You **cannot** pay 付加年金 at the same time. Once you join you cannot cancel voluntarily (verify current rules). Its annuity is not inflation-linked; verify. For most young people iDeCo is more flexible inside the same cap. The Fund itself must make its own change to use the new ¥75k cap; changing iDeCo does not change the Fund amount.
- **Why people miss it:** Few people know it exists, and those who do confuse it with iDeCo.
- **What we may say:** We may show that it shares the iDeCo cap and the deduction maths. Comparing plans for a specific person is FP or insurance-type advice.
- **Freelancer paid from abroad:** nothing
- **Sources:** MHLW iDeCo leaflet (combined ¥68k → ¥75k cap) https://www.mhlw.go.jp/content/12500000/001620594.pdf ; 日本年金機構 FAQ on 付加保険料 (not with 国民年金基金) https://www.nenkin.go.jp/section/faq/kokunen/seido/hokenryo/seidosetsumei/20140414.html
- **Confidence:** The cap is verified primary. The no-cancellation rule and lack of inflation-indexing are secondary/from memory: unknown, verify at npfa.or.jp.

### Extra ¥400 National Pension (付加年金)

- **Group:** insurance & benefits
- **Who it is for:** 第1号被保険者 who pay the full 国民年金 premium and are not in 国民年金基金
- **What it is:** Pay an extra ¥400 a month and you get ¥200 a year more pension for life for every month you paid. It pays for itself after 2 years of pension, and the ¥400 is also tax-deductible.
- **Value:** Pension per year = ¥200 × months paid. Example: 10 years (120 months) costs ¥48,000 and pays ¥24,000 a year for life from 65. After tax relief (~20%) the net cost is about ¥38,400, so it is repaid in about 1.6 years of pension. The ¥400 also uses up ¥1,000 of iDeCo room, because iDeCo amounts go in ¥1,000 steps.
- **Time to money:** years (from 65)
- **Risk:** low. Not inflation-indexed, so its real value shrinks.
- **Effort:** minutes (apply at the 区役所 or 年金事務所)
- **Deadline or season:** Payments start from the month you apply. **You cannot backdate them.** Start as early as possible.
- **Traps and caveats:** Not available if you have any 免除 or 猶予, or if you are in 国民年金基金. Pay it on time or that month lapses.
- **Why people miss it:** ¥400 sounds trivial, and nobody explains the 2-year payback.
- **What we may say:** information only. The maths is simple and factual.
- **Freelancer paid from abroad:** nothing
- **Sources:** 日本年金機構 付加保険料の納付 https://www.nenkin.go.jp/service/kokunen/hokenryo/fukanofu.html ; FAQ https://www.nenkin.go.jp/section/faq/kokunen/seido/hokenryo/seidosetsumei/20140414.html ; leaflet https://www.nenkin.go.jp/service/kokunen/kanyu/20140710-04.files/fuka.pdf
- **Confidence:** verified primary (¥400 a month, ¥200 × months, no backdating, no inflation indexing)

### Prepay 国民年金 for 2 years (国民年金保険料の2年前納)

- **Group:** stop the leak
- **Who it is for:** Anyone paying 国民年金 themselves (freelancers, students paying, the unemployed)
- **What it is:** Pay 24 months up front and you get a discount, about 4% total, which beats any savings account. You can deduct it all in the year paid or split it across the years.
- **Value:** FY2026 premium ¥17,920 a month (FY2027 ¥18,290). 2-year prepayment by **口座振替: ¥417,150, a ¥17,370 discount**. By 納付書 or credit card: ¥418,510 (¥16,010 discount). 1-year prepayment by direct debit saves ¥4,510. Paying by card may also earn card points on about ¥418k.
- **Time to money:** immediate (lower cost)
- **Risk:** none (if you then get a 免除 or join a company, the overpaid part is refunded; verify the process)
- **Effort:** minutes (form at the 年金事務所)
- **Deadline or season:** 2-year prepayment runs April to March. The application deadline is usually end of February for the April start (not shown on the page fetched; verify).
- **Traps and caveats:** It needs a large cash outlay in April. Deducting the whole amount in one year can push you into a lower bracket the following year; choose whichever split saves more.
- **Why people miss it:** The default is monthly paper slips. The discount is not advertised.
- **What we may say:** information only.
- **Freelancer paid from abroad:** nothing
- **Sources:** 日本年金機構 国民年金保険料の前納 (FY2026 table, FY2027 premium ¥18,290) https://www.nenkin.go.jp/service/kokunen/hokenryo/zenno.html
- **Confidence:** Amounts are verified primary. The application deadline is unknown, verify.

### Blue return with e-Tax (青色申告特別控除 ¥650k, ¥750k from 2027)

- **Group:** tax
- **Who it is for:** Anyone with 事業所得 (freelance business income) or real-estate business income who filed 青色申告承認申請書. If you have only ever filed white, apply now.
- **What it is:** If you keep double-entry books and file online on time, you get a free ¥650k deduction. It lowers 所得税, 住民税 **and 国保** (but not 個人事業税). Blue filers also carry losses forward 3 years, can pay family members salary as an expense (青色事業専従者給与), and expense items up to ¥400k at once (see the forgotten-expenses row).
- **Value:** extra deduction × (所得税 marginal × 1.021 + 10% + 国保 rate). Example profile: ¥650k vs white (¥0) × ~30.8% ≈ **¥200k a year**. Versus the ¥100k simple-bookkeeping version: ¥550k × 30.8% ≈ ¥170k. **2027 changes:** ¥750k if you file by e-Tax and keep 優良な電子帳簿; ¥650k with e-Tax; double-entry books filed on paper drop to **¥100k**; simple bookkeeping with revenue over ¥10M two years earlier drops to ¥0.
- **Time to money:** The tax saving comes at the next 確定申告. The 国保 and 住民税 cut comes the following June.
- **Risk:** none
- **Effort:** a weekend to set up accounting software; then ongoing
- **Deadline or season:** 青色申告承認申請書 is due **by Mar 15 of the year** you want it (within 2 months of starting a new business). To get the ¥650k the return must be filed **by Mar 15 of the next year** via e-Tax. A late return gets only ¥100k. For ¥750k in 2027 the electronic-book setup must be in place **from Jan 1, 2027**.
- **Traps and caveats:** Filing one day late costs about ¥170k. Uploading scanned images does not count as e-Tax. If profit is below ¥650k, the deduction is capped at the profit. Paper filers lose the most from 2027. The deduction does not reduce 個人事業税.
- **Why people miss it:** Young freelancers file white "just this once" and stay white. They don't realise it also lowers 国保.
- **What we may say:** We may show the deduction and an estimate. Preparing the return for the user is 税理士 work. Linking to accounting software is fine as information.
- **Freelancer paid from abroad:** Book each USD invoice in yen at the TTM rate of the transaction date, generally. Book the conversion gain or loss when the money arrives, and PayPal fees as expenses. Source for the TTM rule: 所得税基本通達57の3-2 (not re-fetched; verify).
- **Sources:** NTA No.2072 (as of Apr 1, 2026: 55万/65万/10万 and conditions; e-Tax "その年分の確定申告期限（翌年3月15日）までに") https://www.nta.go.jp/taxes/shiraberu/taxanswer/shotoku/2072.htm ; NTA leaflet on the 令和8年度改正 (75万 from 令和9年分; 10万 removed for 簡易簿記 filers with revenue over 1,000万円 two years earlier) https://www.nta.go.jp/taxes/shiraberu/shinkoku/kojin_jigyo/0026004-012.pdf ; NTA 青色申告承認申請手続 https://www.nta.go.jp/taxes/tetsuzuki/shinsei/annai/shinkoku/annai/09.htm ; paper double-entry → 10万 and 住民税 from 令和10年度: Yamada & Partners summary of the 大綱 https://www.yamada-partners.jp/reform/r8/k08-revision-of-special-deduction-for-blue-tax-returns
- **Confidence:** The 2026 rules are verified primary. The 75万 and 簡易簿記 changes for 2027 are verified primary (NTA leaflet). Paper double-entry dropping to ¥100k is secondary only; verify.

### Deduct the business share of home costs (家事按分)

- **Group:** tax
- **Who it is for:** Freelancers who work from home: rent, electricity, internet, phone, and part of a car or bike if used for work.
- **What it is:** If part of your home is used for work, that share of rent and bills is a business expense. You need a reasonable, documented basis, such as floor area or hours of use.
- **Value:** business % × annual cost × ~36% (all-in marginal in the profile). Example: rent ¥120k a month, work room 25% → ¥360k a year; internet ¥5k a month at 60% → ¥36k; phone ¥4k a month at 50% → ¥24k. Total ¥420k × 36% ≈ **¥150k a year** less tax and 国保.
- **Time to money:** The saving comes at the next 確定申告 and 国保 assessment.
- **Risk:** low (if documented); audit risk if you claim 100% of a home
- **Effort:** an evening (measure, write the basis down, keep the lease)
- **Deadline or season:** Expenses are claimed on the return; fix past years via 更正の請求.
- **Traps and caveats:** The NTA says only the part "clearly separable" as directly needed for the business counts. Write down the basis. White filers face a stricter test (the business part must be the main part or clearly separable); commonly cited from 所得税基本通達45-2, not re-fetched, verify. Rent paid to a family member you live with generally is not deductible. Home loan interest and depreciation of an owned home involve different rules.
- **Why people miss it:** Fear of audits and the belief that "home costs are personal".
- **What we may say:** We may show the formula and typical bases. Saying "you can claim 40%" for a specific person is 税務相談 under the 税理士法. Show the formula with the user's inputs instead.
- **Freelancer paid from abroad:** If you work US hours at night, electricity and AC use rise; time-based apportionment can reflect that.
- **Sources:** NTA No.2210 (as of Apr 1, 2026) "家事関連費のうち必要経費になるのは…業務遂行上直接必要であったことが明らかに区分できる場合のその区分できる金額に限られます" https://www.nta.go.jp/taxes/shiraberu/taxanswer/shotoku/2210.htm
- **Confidence:** The rule is verified primary. The white-filer 50% rule is secondary; verify. Percentages are illustrative.

### Expenses freelancers forget (経費の漏れ) and the ¥400k instant write-off

- **Group:** tax
- **Who it is for:** All 事業所得 filers. The ¥400k write-off is for 青色 filers only.
- **What it is:** Anything you spend to earn the income is an expense, and young freelancers routinely miss a lot of it. Examples: software subscriptions (Adobe, Figma, fonts, stock images), PayPal and Wise fees and FX conversion losses, a laptop or monitor, coworking, books and courses, client meals, travel to client meetings, domain and hosting, 個人事業税 (deductible in the year paid), bank fees, accounting software, the share of a tablet or phone. 青色 filers can expense each item **under ¥400k (acquired from Apr 1, 2026; was ¥300k) in full in one year, up to ¥3M a year, through Mar 31, 2029**. Anyone can expense items under ¥100k, or spread items of ¥100k–¥200k over 3 years.
- **Value:** missed expenses × ~36% (所得税 + 住民税 + 国保 + 事業税). Example: a ¥350k MacBook expensed at once (blue) instead of depreciated over 4 years brings ¥262k of deductions forward, so about ¥94k of tax comes a year earlier. ¥100k of forgotten subscriptions ≈ ¥36k.
- **Time to money:** next 確定申告
- **Risk:** low (if real and receipted)
- **Effort:** an evening a month (or connect cards to accounting software)
- **Deadline or season:** Buy before Dec 31 to count this year (the item must be put into use).
- **Traps and caveats:** Buying things you don't need "for tax" still costs you the other 64%. Mixed-use items need 按分. Under tax-inclusive accounting, the size test for each item includes consumption tax.
- **Why people miss it:** PayPal fees and FX losses are invisible because they are netted off before payout. People also think only "big" things count.
- **What we may say:** We may list categories and the rules. Do not decide for a user whether a specific item is deductible.
- **Freelancer paid from abroad:** PayPal's fee and its built-in FX spread are real expenses or FX losses. Record the gross USD invoice as revenue at TTM, then the fee and the conversion loss separately. Under-recording fees overstates income.
- **Sources:** NTA No.2100 (as of Apr 1, 2026): 10万円未満 full expense; 一括償却 10–20万 over 3 years; 青色/中小 special, "令和8年3月31日まで…30万円未満", after that "40万円未満", "300万円に達するまで", through 令和11年3月31日 https://www.nta.go.jp/taxes/shiraberu/taxanswer/shotoku/2100.htm ; Tokyo 個人事業税 page https://www.tax.metro.tokyo.lg.jp/kazei/kojin_ji.html
- **Confidence:** The write-off thresholds are verified primary. The examples are illustrative.

### The ¥4.89M line and other 2026–27 deduction changes (基礎控除の引上げ, 年収の壁)

- **Group:** tax
- **Who it is for:** Every Japanese resident filing income tax. The cliff matters most for freelancers whose 合計所得 lands near ¥4.89M.
- **What it is:** The 2026 tax reform raised the income-tax basic deduction. For 2026 and 2027 income it is **¥1.04M if 合計所得 ≤ ¥4.89M, ¥670k for ¥4.89M–6.55M, ¥620k for ¥6.55M–23.5M**. 2025 had five steps (¥950k/880k/680k/630k/580k). From 2028 it is scheduled at ¥990k (≤ ¥1.32M) and ¥620k otherwise, unless extended. The tax-free threshold for wage earners is now ¥1.78M. Dependants may now earn up to 合計所得 ¥620k (was ¥580k). **住民税 still uses ¥430k**, so the increase affects 所得税 only. From Jan 2027 there is a new 1% 防衛特別所得税 on income tax, but the 復興特別所得税 rate falls by 1 point, so the net effect is about zero.
- **Value:** Crossing ¥4.89M costs ¥370k of deduction × 所得税 marginal × 1.021, which is **¥38k (10% band) to ¥76k (20% band)**. Example: revenue ¥6.0M, expenses ¥300k, 青色 ¥650k gives 合計所得 ¥5.05M, deduction ¥670k, 20% band. Recording ¥160k more genuine expenses (or the full 青色 ¥650k) brings it to ¥4.89M, so the deduction becomes ¥1.04M and saves up to about ¥76k (less where the taxable income also drops into the 10% band) on top of the expenses' own saving. Note that iDeCo and 共済 do **not** reduce 合計所得. Only expenses, 青色控除 and losses do.
- **Time to money:** the 2026 return (filed Feb–Mar 2027)
- **Risk:** none
- **Effort:** minutes to check
- **Deadline or season:** Dec 31 for expenses. Applies to 2026 and 2027 income.
- **Traps and caveats:** The ¥1.04M amount is temporary (2026–27). 合計所得 includes other income such as side wages, and FX gains if you hold USD privately. Non-residents get only the main ¥620k.
- **Why people miss it:** The news says "¥1.78M wall" in terms of wages. Freelancers don't realise the bands are based on 合計所得 and that one band covers most ¥5–7M-revenue freelancers.

### When to incorporate (法人成り)

- **Group:** tax
- **Who it is for:** Freelancers whose profit keeps rising. It is usually not yet worth it at ¥6M revenue.
- **What it is:** Turning your business into a one-person company. You then pay yourself a salary, which gets the salary deduction, and the company pays corporate tax (15% on the first ¥8M for small companies) instead of you paying up to 45% income tax. But you take on fixed costs and mandatory employee-style social insurance.
- **Value:** It needs a side-by-side calculation. Inputs: profit, salary split, 社会保険 (health + 厚生年金, roughly 30% of salary split between company and you), 法人住民税均等割 (about ¥70k a year minimum in Tokyo, verify), and accountant fees (anecdotally ¥200k–500k a year). The commonly cited breakeven is profit of ¥8M–10M+; that range is secondary only. Example: at 事業所得 ¥4.35M your marginal 所得税 is 10%, below the 15% corporate rate, so incorporating adds cost.
- **Time to money:** years
- **Risk:** low tax risk, behavioural risk (admin load); 厚生年金 increases future pension
- **Effort:** a weekend to set up, then ongoing
- **Deadline or season:** usually at a year start
- **Traps and caveats:** The 2026 deduction increases raise the breakeven for sole proprietors. The 小規模企業共済 payout on closing the sole proprietorship can be taken as retirement income when you incorporate. A new company is not automatically consumption-tax exempt if you register for invoices.
- **Why people miss it:** They do it too early because of hype, or too late.
- **What we may say:** We may show the general formula and inputs. A recommendation to incorporate for a specific user is 税理士 territory.
- **Freelancer paid from abroad:** US clients may need a new W-8BEN-E for a company, and you would need a business bank or PayPal account. Banks can be slow to open accounts for new companies with foreign income.

### Consumption tax refund for export freelancers (輸出免税 + 課税事業者選択)

- **Group:** tax
- **Who it is for:** Freelancers whose clients are mostly abroad (non-residents) and who pay meaningful Japanese consumption tax on business expenses. They are usually below the ¥10M threshold and therefore exempt by default.
- **What it is:** Services to non-resident clients are usually 0%-rated exports (輸出免税), not exempt. So if you choose to become a consumption-tax payer, you charge 0% on exports but can claim back the 10% you paid on Japanese expenses, and you get a refund. As a default 免税事業者 you can never recover that 10%.
- **Value:** refund ≈ consumption tax paid on taxable domestic expenses (10/110 of tax-inclusive spending; rent for a home is 非課税 and gives nothing). Example: laptop ¥330k + software ¥110k + coworking ¥220k + phone/internet ¥88k + travel and other ¥352k = ¥1.1M → **≈ ¥100k a year refund**. Under tax-inclusive accounting the refund counts as income.
- **Time to money:** weeks after the consumption tax return (due Mar 31)
- **Risk:** low (bookkeeping errors). Behavioural: the 2-year lock-in.
- **Effort:** an evening to file; then ongoing (keep qualifying invoices for every purchase)
- **Deadline or season:** File 消費税課税事業者選択届出書 **by the day before the period starts**, so by **Dec 31, 2026 for 2027**. You cannot withdraw for 2 years. If you buy a fixed asset of ¥1M+ (excluding tax) during those years and use general taxation, you are locked in for 3 years and cannot use 簡易課税.
- **Traps and caveats:** It only pays if your domestic sales are small. Japanese clients' sales become taxable at 10% (you would charge them 10%). **Never choose 簡易課税** as an exporter: it gives no refund. Services consumed in Japan (e.g., lodging or meals for a non-resident) are not exempt. Services that count as 電気通信利用役務の提供 (e.g., ongoing consulting by email, online content distribution) follow the customer's location and are out of scope (不課税), not 0%-rated exports, which changes the maths. Commissioned design or production delivered online is excluded from that category per NTA guidance. Keep proof that the client is a non-resident (contracts, invoices, address).
- **Why people miss it:** "Exempt" sounds better than "taxable", and nobody tells freelancers that exports are refundable.
- **What we may say:** We may explain the mechanism and estimate the refund from the user's expense data. Classifying a specific service, or filing for the user, is 税理士 work. Prompt them to confirm with the 税務署 or a 税理士.
- **Freelancer paid from abroad:** This is the core case. USD sales to US clients count toward the ¥10M taxable-sales test (export sales are taxable sales at 0%), so watch the threshold two years ahead. PayPal fees are generally non-taxable financial fees (no input credit); verify.
- **Sources:** NTA No.6551 輸出取引の免税 (as of Apr 1, 2026; services to non-residents exempt except "国内における飲食・宿泊" etc.; non-resident definition) https://www.nta.go.jp/taxes/shiraberu/taxanswer/shohi/6551.htm ; NTA No.6501 納税義務の免除 https://www.nta.go.jp/taxes/shiraberu/taxanswer/shohi/6501.htm ; NTA No.6629 届出書 https://www.nta.go.jp/taxes/shiraberu/taxanswer/shohi/6629.htm ; NTA 質疑応答 課税事業者選択の取りやめと簡易課税制度選択の制限 https://www.nta.go.jp/law/shitsugi/shohi/22/11.htm ; NTA 基本通達 5-8 (電気通信利用役務 exclusions, e.g., software produced on request and delivered online) https://www.nta.go.jp/law/tsutatsu/kihon/shohi/05/08.htm ; NTA cross-border Q&A (revised Jun 2026) https://www.nta.go.jp/publication/pamph/pdf/cross-QA.pdf

### Invoice registration: skip it if your clients are abroad; use the 2割/3割 relief if you register (インボイス制度)

- **Group:** stop the leak
- **Who it is for:** Freelancers deciding whether to register as インボイス発行事業者, and those already registered
- **What it is:** Registering makes you a consumption-tax payer. Overseas clients don't need Japanese invoices, so an export-only freelancer gains nothing from registering. The "export refund" row is a cleaner route if you want refunds. If you have Japanese business clients who demand invoices, registration plus the special reliefs keeps the cost low. **2割特例** (pay 20% of output tax) applies through your **2026 return**. A new **3割特例** (30%) applies for individuals for **2027 and 2028**. You choose at filing, so compare with general taxation, which gives a refund if most sales are exports.
- **Value:** Example: ¥1.0M of Japanese client sales + ¥5.0M of US sales; output tax ¥100k on the Japanese portion. 2割特例 = ¥20k payable. 3割特例 (2027–28) = ¥30k. General taxation = ¥100k − input tax on all expenses (e.g., ¥100k) = about ¥0 or a refund. Not registering = ¥0 tax, but Japanese clients may cut fees by about 10%. From Oct 1, 2026 they can still deduct only **70%** of the tax on purchases from unregistered sellers (was 80%), falling to 50% from Oct 2028, 30% from Oct 2030, and 0 from Oct 2031.
- **Time to money:** yearly
- **Risk:** low
- **Effort:** an evening
- **Deadline or season:** The 2割/3割 choice is made on the return. To switch to 簡易課税 after using 2割/3割, file by that year's return deadline.
- **Traps and caveats:** Registering by default "because everyone does" adds a filing duty for no benefit. Once registered, de-registration takes time; verify the procedure. The 3割特例 applies only to individuals who became taxpayers because they registered.
- **Why people miss it:** The 2023 invoice panic made everyone register.
- **What we may say:** We may compare the options numerically for the user. Recommending registration is advice; show trade-offs instead.
- **Freelancer paid from abroad:** US clients never need a Japanese invoice number.

### Pension exemption or deferral, and catching up later (国民年金の免除・納付猶予・追納)

- **Group:** insurance & benefits
- **Who it is for:** People with low prior-year income (e.g., a first freelance year, students, the unemployed, someone who closed a business). Deferral is for ages 20–49.
- **What it is:** Instead of leaving 国民年金 unpaid (which can lead to seizure and loss of disability pension cover), apply for full, ¾, half or ¼ exemption or deferral. A full exemption still counts as **half** a paid month toward your pension. You can pay back (追納) within 10 years and deduct it then, ideally in a high-income year.
- **Value:** Full exemption saves ¥17,920 a month (¥215k a year in FY2026) of cash now and still earns half the pension for those months. Catching up later converts the months into full credit and gives a deduction at a higher bracket. Example: catch up 12 months (~¥215k + surcharge) in a 20%-band year → about ¥65k tax saved that year. Surcharges apply from the 3rd fiscal year after the exemption.
- **Time to money:** weeks (approval)
- **Risk:** low (pension slightly smaller if never caught up)
- **Effort:** minutes (online via マイナポータル or at the 区役所)
- **Deadline or season:** Apply each July for the July–June year. You can backdate up to **2 years and 1 month**.
- **Traps and caveats:** Partial exemption only counts if you pay the remaining part. You cannot use iDeCo or 付加年金 while exempt (iDeCo eligibility: verify). Income test: full exemption if (dependants + 1) × ¥350k + ¥320k or less. Business closure or job loss lets you apply under a special rule without the income test.
- **Why people miss it:** People simply stop paying, which is the worst option. They don't know exemption still earns pension.
- **What we may say:** information and eligibility checks. Fine to show.
- **Freelancer paid from abroad:** A weak yen can raise your prior-year income above the line; check each year.
- **Sources:** 日本年金機構 免除・納付猶予 (updated Oct 16, 2026) https://www.nenkin.go.jp/service/kokunen/menjo/20150428.html ; 追納 https://www.nenkin.go.jp/service/kokunen/menjo/20150331.html

### Lower your 国保 premium (国民健康保険料の軽減・減免)

- **Group:** stop the leak
- **Who it is for:** Freelancers on 国民健康保険, especially in low-income or loss years, people who lost a job involuntarily, and parents (pre-school children, around childbirth)
- **What it is:** 国保 is charged on last year's income minus only ¥430k. Most other deductions (iDeCo, 共済, medical costs) are ignored, while business expenses and 青色控除 do count. Low-income households get 70/50/20% off the flat per-person part **automatically, but only if a 住民税 return is on file, even showing zero income**. There are separate reductions for involuntary job loss, childbirth (from Jan 2024) and pre-school children.

Reduction thresholds (FY2026): 70% off if income ≤ ¥430k; 50% if ≤ ¥430k + ¥310k × members; 20% if ≤ ¥430k + ¥570k × members. Example: a ¥4.35M freelancer pays ≈ ¥482k. Every ¥100k of extra genuine expenses or 青色控除 cuts it by ≈ ¥10.6k.

- **Risk:** none
- **Effort:** minutes
- **Deadline or season:** File the 住民税申告 or 確定申告 by mid-March. Premiums are assessed from June.
- **Traps and caveats:** Skipping the 住民税申告 in a zero-income year means **no automatic reduction**. iDeCo and 共済 do not lower 国保. Rates differ by 区 and city. The new 子ども・子育て支援金 levy rises each year to FY2028.
- **Why people miss it:** Bills arrive with no explanation of the formula.
- **What we may say:** We may estimate the premium with the user's 区 rates (show the assumption).
- **Freelancer paid from abroad:** The premium is based on yen income, so FX swings move next year's premium.

- **Group:** tax
- **Who it is for:** Any taxpayer who paid medical costs for themselves or household family (生計を一にする)

### Pay tax by card or QR for points: only if points exceed the fee (クレジットカード・スマホアプリ納付)

- **Group:** stop the leak
- **Who it is for:** Anyone paying 所得税, 消費税 or 住民税 directly
- **What it is:** National tax can be paid by credit card (with a fee) or by smartphone payment apps (no fee, limited amount). Points beat the fee only if the card's point rate is higher than the fee, or if the spending unlocks a card bonus.
- **Value:** points earned − fee. National tax card fee: historically about 0.83% (¥83 per ¥10k, tax included). Not confirmed this session because the payment site refused connections; unknown, verify. A 1% card nets about +0.17%; a 0.5% card loses money. App payment (PayPay and similar; per-payment cap about ¥300k, verify) often earns no points on tax. 国民年金 can be paid by card with no fee (年金機構 table above).
- **Time to money:** monthly
- **Risk:** low
- **Effort:** minutes
- **Deadline or season:** each due date
- **Traps and caveats:** Card payment is not instant proof of payment. Fees are non-refundable. Revolving or minimum-payment plans destroy any gain. Some cards exclude tax payments from points.
- **Why people miss it:** They either pay a fee for less in points, or ignore the free card route for 国民年金.
- **What we may say:** We may compare a user's card rate to the fee. Recommending a specific card is promotion and may need disclosure.

### Business Safety Mutual Aid as a tax deferral (経営セーフティ共済 / 中小企業倒産防止共済)

- **Group:** tax
- **Who it is for:** Sole proprietors with stable profit who want to move taxable income to a future low year (eligibility, e.g., a minimum business history, unknown, verify)
- **What it is:** You pay ¥5,000–¥200,000 a month and it is a **business expense** now, so unlike iDeCo it lowers 国保 and 合計所得. If you cancel after 40+ months you get 100% back, and that refund is business income in the year received. Use it to shift income to a lower year (e.g., retirement, a gap year, or the year you close the business). It also gives cheap loans if a client goes bankrupt (up to 10× contributions, max ¥80M).
- **Value:** Tax now at ~36% vs tax later at the rate in the cancellation year. Example: ¥600k a year for 4 years = ¥2.4M deducted (~¥860k of tax and 国保 deferred). Received in a ¥0-income year, it might be taxed at 15–20%. If received in a high year, the benefit is near zero.
- **Time to money:** years
- **Risk:** behavioural (cancel within 12 months = nothing back; within 40 months = less than 100%)
- **Effort:** an evening
- **Deadline or season:** Contributions count when paid. 1 year can be prepaid (verify the deduction rule for individuals).
- **Traps and caveats:** **If you cancel on or after Oct 1, 2024 and rejoin within 2 years, contributions are not deductible.** The cancellation refund lands as income in one lump. Total contribution cap of ¥8M is from memory (unknown, verify). Mostly useful above ¥6M revenue.
- **Why people miss it:** It is marketed as bankruptcy insurance, not as a deferral tool.
- **What we may say:** We may describe the mechanism and the user's numbers. Do not recommend it as a "tax-saving product"; it defers tax, it does not remove it.
- **Freelancer paid from abroad:** US-client bankruptcy may not qualify for the loan (it is designed for 取引先 in Japan; verify). The deferral works the same.
- **Sources:** 中小機構 制度の概要 (¥5,000–¥200,000; 必要経費; 10× up to ¥80M; 12-month and 40-month refund rules; 2024 rejoin rule) https://www.smrj.go.jp/kyosai/tkyosai/features/index.html
- **Confidence:** Contribution range, refund rules and the 2024 rule are verified primary. The ¥8M cap, eligibility and coverage of foreign clients are unknown, verify.

### Stop US tax being withheld from your pay (W-8BEN) and claim foreign tax credit if it was (外国税額控除)

- **Group:** get paid better
- **Who it is for:** Japan-resident freelancers paid by US companies or platforms
- **What it is:** US payers may withhold 30% if you don't prove you are a foreign person. A W-8BEN (individual) given to the payer, never to the IRS, establishes that. Services performed outside the US are generally not US-source income, and the Japan–US treaty also applies; verify. If US tax was withheld anyway, Japan's 外国税額控除 or a US refund claim may recover it.
- **Value:** Up to 30% of affected payments. Example: a $1,000 job wrongly withheld = $300 that you need to recover.
- **Time to money:** immediate (prevention); months (recovery)
- **Risk:** low
- **Effort:** minutes per client
- **Deadline or season:** before the first payment
- **Traps and caveats:** Don't tick treaty boxes you don't understand. Some platforms ask for a US tax ID (TIN) or a Japanese My Number as a foreign TIN; verify which. Recovering withheld US tax through Japan's credit is limited (credit limit rules; verify).
- **Why people miss it:** Clients send the form without explanation, or don't send it and withhold.
- **What we may say:** We may explain what the form is for. Filling it in for the user or giving treaty positions is tax advice.
- **Freelancer paid from abroad:** This is the core case.
- **Sources:** IRS Instructions for Form W-8BEN ("Establish that you are not a U.S. person"; "Do not send Form W-8BEN to the IRS") https://www.irs.gov/instructions/iw8ben
