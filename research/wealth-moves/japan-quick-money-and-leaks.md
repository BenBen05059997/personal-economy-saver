# Japan: quick money, points, and wealth destroyers

Researched Oct 31, 2026. Scope: legal quick money (self-back, point sites, sign-up bonuses, bill payment points, account bonuses, high-interest banks), wealth destroyers, and unclaimed money, for anyone living in Japan. Notes for a freelancer paid in USD via PayPal sit in each row.

## Summary (10 lines)

1. **Month one, realistic:** a first-timer who opens 1–2 free securities or bank accounts, gets 1 annual-fee-free card and does 1 FX self-back typically **books about ¥20,000–¥60,000** in rewards. Examples on Oct 31, 2026: A8.net みんなのFX ¥22,000 (needs trades), ハピタス 三井住友カード(NL) 15,000pt, ハピタス 楽天証券 20,000pt, A8 みずほ銀行 ¥7,000.
2. **The cash arrives later:** rewards confirm in about 1 month and pay out 1–2 months after that, so expect the money in **month 2–4**, not month one. Blog claims of "¥100k–¥300k" add up dozens of one-time offers, and each offer works once per person (anecdotal).
3. **Self-back runs out:** each card, account or service pays once per person or household. After that, point sites earn roughly 0.5–2% extra on purchases you would make anyway.
4. **Card-application limit:** credit bureaus (CIC, JICC) keep every application for **6 months**. Common guidance (secondary, not an official rule) is no more than 1–2 card applications a month and to avoid 3 or more within 6 months.
5. **Biggest drain #1, revolving credit (リボ払い):** 17.64% APR on 楽天カード, and 18.0% for new 三井住友カード members from Mar 31, 2026. On a ¥300,000 balance paid at ¥10,000 a month you pay about ¥99,000 in interest over 40 months. At ¥6,000 a month it is about ¥246,000 over 7.7 years (our calculation).
6. **Biggest drain #2, currency conversion on foreign income:** PayPal adds 3–4% to the exchange rate when it converts a USD balance to yen. On $40,000 a year that is about $1,200–$1,600, roughly ¥180k–¥240k at an illustrative ¥150 per dollar.
7. **Biggest drain #3, phone plans and insurance:** ドコモ MAX unlimited costs ¥8,448 a month against ahamo 30GB at ¥2,970, a gap of about ¥66k a year. Households of two or more pay an average of ¥353k a year in life-insurance premiums, while 高額療養費 already caps big medical bills.
8. **Easy wins for freelancers:** pay 国民年金 two years ahead (saves ¥16,010 by card or ¥17,370 by bank debit for FY2026; the card form must reach the pension office by end of February), and add 付加年金 (¥400 a month, pays for itself after 2 years of pension).
9. **Unclaimed money:** dormant bank accounts can be withdrawn at any time, with no deadline. ねんきんネット can search for lost pension records. Old employer DC money that was auto-transferred earns nothing and pays fees. Overpaid tax can be reclaimed for **5 years** (更正の請求 / 還付申告).
10. **Deadlines now:** 東京都 018サポート application for the December payment closes **Nov 1, 2026**. 持続化補助金 round 20 closes **Dec 15, 2026, 17:00** (様式4 by Dec 4). デジタル化・AI導入補助金2026 final grant application closes **Jan 7, 2027**.

Research note: the shared web-search budget ran out partway through. Several figures below are marked "unknown, verify" rather than filled from memory: Paidy late fees, cash-exchange spreads, Wise's USD→JPY fee, the round-20 持続化補助金 cap and rate, and the 休眠預金 yearly totals.

## Quick money

### Self-back on affiliate networks (セルフバック)

- **Group:** quick money
- **Who it is for:** adults in Japan with a bank account who register as a free "media member" of an affiliate network (A8.net, もしもアフィリエイト, afb, アクセストレード). A8 says self-back is for the registered person only. Family members, companies and resale are not allowed.
- **What it is:** advertisers pay networks a fee for each new customer. With self-back the network passes that fee to you when you sign up or buy yourself, through a special self-back page. You get paid for things like opening a bank account, getting a card or switching your internet line. Each offer works once per person.
- **Value:** reward per offer, minus any money you must spend or risk, minus the transfer fee. Inputs: which offers you have not used yet. Example from A8.net's self-back page (Oct 31, 2026): 楽天カード ¥5,750, みずほ銀行 account plus online-banking registration ¥7,000, au PAY カード ¥3,000, コインチェック account plus a purchase ¥7,850. Taking all four books **¥23,600**, less a transfer fee of ¥66 (ゆうちょ) to ¥660 (other banks, ¥30k or more).
- **Time to money:** weeks to months. A8 pays when your confirmed balance passes ¥1,000: rewards confirmed by the end of a month are paid on the 15th two months later. Confirmation itself takes about 1 month, so the total is often 2–3 months (secondary).
- **Risk:** low. The main risk is behavioural: signing up for credit products you then use badly.
- **Effort:** an evening for the first batch, then minutes per offer
- **Deadline or season:** offers change and expire without notice. Some must be completed within 30–90 days (deposit or trade deadlines).
- **Traps and caveats:** the reward is cancelled if you close the browser midway, have cookies disabled, apply by phone or in a store, or withdraw or close the account before approval. Same-household applications are refused. A8 can suspend or expel members for applying to services they don't plan to use. Loan products appear as offers (楽天銀行スーパーローン ¥20,625, フクホー ¥1,904); a loan application stays on your credit file for 6 months, so skip them. Tax: most tax commentary treats self-back as taxable income (雑所得 or 一時所得). The category is unsettled; a single 税理士ドットコム answer said 一時所得, which gets a ¥500k special deduction and half-taxation.
- **Why people miss it:** it is marketed as a blogger tool, and few people outside the affiliate world know you may use it yourself.
- **What we may say:** we can explain the mechanism, the payout timing and the one-time nature, and add up a user's own claimed rewards. Pushing specific financial products in return for affiliate commission needs an "広告" label under the 景品表示法 stealth-marketing rule (in force since Oct 1, 2023). For securities, compensated introductions can become 金融サービス仲介業 or 金融商品仲介業. Telling one person how to classify their income for tax is 税理士 work under 税理士法.
- **Freelancer paid from abroad:** you file a 確定申告 anyway, so the "under ¥200k no filing" rule does not apply to you. That rule only covers salary earners (NTA No.1900). Report the rewards. Resident tax has no ¥200k exemption either (secondary, verify with your 区役所).
- **Sources:** A8.net self-back LP, reward amounts https://support.a8.net/selfback/lp/index.php (Oct 31, 2026; page undated); A8 self-back cautions https://support.a8.net/as/caution.html (Oct 31, 2026); A8 FAQ "セルフバックとは" https://support.a8.net/a8/as/faq/2008/03/post_161.html and payout methods https://support.a8.net/as/payment/ (Oct 31, 2026; minimum ¥1,000, 15th of the month after next, fees ¥66–¥660 as given in search summary of the official pages, some FAQ pages date from 2008); NTA No.1900 https://www.nta.go.jp/taxes/shiraberu/taxanswer/shotoku/1900.htm; NTA No.1490 一時所得 https://www.nta.go.jp/taxes/shiraberu/taxanswer/shotoku/1490.htm; 税理士ドットコム Q&A https://www.zeiri4.com/c_5/q_146642/ (individual answer, not official); takapon.net on timing https://takapon.net/selfback-open-securities-account/ (dated Jul 31, 2026)
- **Confidence:** verified primary for the A8 rewards, rules and payout minimum. Secondary for the transfer-fee table (official A8 pages read through a search summary; some FAQs are old). Anecdotal for the tax classification and the 2–3 month total wait.

### FX account self-back (FX口座開設＋取引のセルフバック)

- **Group:** quick money
- **Who it is for:** adults with ¥50,000–¥100,000 of spare cash they can tie up for 1–3 months, and the discipline to trade the minimum and stop
- **What it is:** FX brokers pay the largest self-back rewards, but you must make trades, often a set volume within 30–90 days. You open and close positions straight away to meet the condition, paying the spread each time. Then you stop.
- **Value:** reward − (spread × volume) − trading losses. Example: A8.net lists みんなのFX "新規口座開設＋取引 ¥22,000" and LIGHT FX ¥18,000 (Oct 31, 2026). On 10,000-currency round trips at a 0.2-sen spread the cost is about ¥20 per round trip, but a price move in the seconds between buy and sell can be ±several hundred yen. Some offers require 30 lots (60 lots round trip) within 90 days (ハピタス / モッピー みんなのFX, ¥3,000), so volume costs vary a lot by route. Read the exact condition.
- **Time to money:** about 1 month to approve plus 1–2 months to pay, so 2–3 months total
- **Risk:** market (small but real losses while trading) and behavioural (some people keep trading after the offer)
- **Effort:** an evening, plus a few minutes per trade day
- **Deadline or season:** trades must be completed within the window, often 30–90 days
- **Traps and caveats:** withdrawing funds or closing the account before approval cancels the reward. One per person and per household. Heavy-volume offers (e.g., 300,000 currency units) are risky for beginners. Don't try to win back losses. FX gains are taxable (separately taxed 20.315% on futures-type FX; verify).
- **Why people miss it:** "FX" sounds like gambling, so people skip the one offer type that pays ¥10k–¥20k+. Others jump in without reading the volume condition.
- **What we may say:** we can show the reward, the stated condition and an estimated spread cost. We cannot recommend a currency pair, trade direction or position size; that is 投資助言 under the 金融商品取引法.
- **Freelancer paid from abroad:** nothing. Do not use your USD business float for this.
- **Sources:** A8.net self-back LP https://support.a8.net/selfback/lp/index.php (Oct 31, 2026); takapon.net, trade requirements, spread, cancellation rules https://takapon.net/selfback-open-securities-account/ (Jul 31, 2026); enma-blog, みんなのFX conditions by route https://enma-blog.com/selfback-min-fx/ (updated Aug 24, 2025)
- **Confidence:** verified primary for the A8 reward amounts. Anecdotal for the trade conditions, spread estimate and timing (blogs only; check the live offer page).

### Securities and bank account opening rewards (証券・銀行口座開設の特典)

- **Group:** quick money
- **Who it is for:** adults resident in Japan with a My Number card or notice
- **What it is:** online brokers and banks pay for new customers, either through point sites and affiliate self-back or through their own campaigns. Opening a free account and sometimes depositing ¥50,000 earns a one-time reward. Keep the accounts only if they are useful, for example for NISA or a high-interest savings account.
- **Value:** reward − any deposit opportunity cost. Example on ハピタス (Oct 31, 2026): 楽天証券 20,000pt, SBI証券 18,000pt (new account plus a ¥50,000 deposit), 三菱UFJ eスマート証券 16,000pt (first trade). A8.net: みずほ銀行 ¥7,000. 1 ハピタス point is about ¥1 (cash withdrawal is 500pt for ¥500 plus a 100pt fee, or free through some gift routes).
- **Time to money:** weeks to months
- **Risk:** none to low. Market risk only if the condition needs a trade.
- **Effort:** an evening
- **Deadline or season:** campaign-dependent. Point-site values move week to week.
- **Traps and caveats:** you can open a NISA account at only one institution per year, so chasing a broker reward can mean moving NISA, which has a timing cost. Each broker or bank pays once per person, and you must use one route per account (A8 or ハピタス, not both). The conditions (deposit amount, trade, deadline) differ between routes. Every bank and broker account you open is another password and another notice to keep track of.
- **Why people miss it:** people open accounts directly on the brand's site and leave ¥10k–¥20k on the table
- **What we may say:** factual comparison of the stated rewards and conditions is fine. Telling a specific user which broker to choose, especially for an affiliate fee, risks 金融サービス仲介業 / 投資助言 issues.
- **Freelancer paid from abroad:** brokers ask for occupation and income source; foreign-currency business income is fine, but have your 開業届 or tax return ready if asked (verify per broker).
- **Sources:** ハピタス top page offers https://hapitas.jp/ (Oct 31, 2026); ハピタス exchange page https://hapitas.jp/exchange/ (Oct 31, 2026); A8 LP https://support.a8.net/selfback/lp/index.php (Oct 31, 2026); muroyaaffiliate, A8 securities and bank list https://muroyaaffiliate.co.jp/a8net-selfback-anken-osusume/ (updated Jun 4, 2026; DMM.com証券 ¥10,000, 楽天証券 ¥391–¥8,500, 三菱UFJ銀行 ¥4,000)
- **Confidence:** verified primary for the ハピタス and A8 figures on Oct 31, 2026. Secondary for the muroyaaffiliate list. The NISA one-institution rule is from general knowledge; verify on the 金融庁 NISA site.

### Credit card sign-up bonuses, stacked with a point site (クレカ入会キャンペーン＋ポイントサイト経由)

- **Group:** quick money
- **Who it is for:** adults with a clean credit file who will pay the full balance every month. Applicants need stable income; self-employed approval criteria are not published (unknown, verify).
- **What it is:** card issuers give new members points, and point sites pay a separate reward when you apply through them. Many people can earn both on the same card. Pick annual-fee-free cards so there is no cost to keep or cancel.
- **Value:** issuer bonus + point-site reward − any spending you would not otherwise do. Example: 楽天カード's own offer is 5,000 points (2,000 on joining plus 3,000 for using the card 3 times; requires 楽天e-NAVI registration and a direct-debit setup). ハピタス pays 10,000pt for 楽天カード, so **about ¥15,000 for one free card** (Oct 31, 2026). Other ハピタス values the same day: 三井住友カード(NL) 15,000pt, 三菱UFJカード 15,000pt, エポスカード 12,000pt, JCB CARD W 5,000pt. Whether you can stack the issuer campaign and the point site depends on each offer's terms (verify).
- **Time to money:** weeks to about 2–3 months
- **Risk:** behavioural. A new credit line plus "spend X to unlock" conditions tempts overspending.
- **Effort:** an evening per card
- **Deadline or season:** campaigns change without notice; see each issuer's period
- **Traps and caveats:** **申込みブラック**: CIC and JICC keep every application for 6 months from the date the issuer checked your file. Common (unofficial) guidance is no more than 1–2 cards a month, and a rejection means waiting 6 months. Points-for-revolving campaigns ("register for リボ and get X points") exist; never take them (see the リボ row). Watch for annual fees after year one on "first year free" cards. Spending thresholds can be met by paying tax or 国民年金 by card (see below). Ethics: applying only to cancel straight away is allowed by most terms but can get you blocked by that issuer. A8 bans applying to "services you don't plan to use".
- **Why people miss it:** people apply through the issuer's ad or in a store and skip the point site, losing ¥5k–¥15k per card
- **What we may say:** we can list issuer and point-site offers and the 6-month credit-file rule. We may not tell a specific user they will be approved, or recommend credit, as a paid intermediary without 割賦販売法 or 金融サービス仲介業 compliance (verify with counsel).
- **Freelancer paid from abroad:** you will be asked for annual income; use your last 確定申告 figure in yen. Approval for freelancers in their first year can be harder (anecdotal). Apply while you still have salary history if you are about to go freelance.
- **Sources:** 楽天カード top page https://www.rakuten-card.co.jp/ (Oct 31, 2026); ハピタス https://hapitas.jp/ (Oct 31, 2026); 三井住友カード(NL) https://www.smbc-card.com/nyukai/card/numberless.jsp (Oct 31, 2026; annual fee 永年無料, campaign amount not shown in text); CIC retention FAQ https://www.cic.co.jp/faq/detail/1105.html and https://www.cic.co.jp/confidence/posession.html (申込情報 kept 6 months from the 照会日); JICC https://www.jicc.co.jp/credit_info/registration/ (6 months); d-money magazine on application limits https://magazine.d-money.jp/creditcard/simultaneous-application/ and crexgroup https://crexgroup.com/ja/creditcard/application/credit-card-application-interval-tips/ (secondary, "2 at once" guidance)
- **Confidence:** verified primary for the 楽天 bonus, the ハピタス values and the 6-month retention (CIC/JICC pages read through official-domain search results). Secondary for the "1–2 a month" guidance, which is not an official threshold.

### Point sites for everyday shopping (ポイントサイト経由のポイ活)

- **Group:** quick money
- **Who it is for:** anyone who shops online (楽天市場, Yahoo!, travel bookings, subscriptions, insurance quotes)
- **What it is:** sites like モッピー, ハピタス and ポイントインカム get a referral fee when you click through them before buying, and share part of it with you as points. It is the same purchase at the same price, with an extra rebate on top. The big money is one-off sign-ups; everyday routing adds a little extra on what you already spend.
- **Value:** extra points = online spend through the site × rebate rate. Monthly online spend × 1% (an illustrative rate; rates vary by shop and day, verify) is about ¥300 a month on ¥30,000. Values: モッピー 1P = ¥1 with free exchange; ポイントインカム 10pt = ¥1 (secondary); ハピタス cash withdrawal 500pt for ¥500 with a 100pt fee, gift-card routes free.
- **Time to money:** weeks to months (shop approval often takes 1–3 months; varies)
- **Risk:** low. Tracking can fail.
- **Effort:** ongoing (seconds per purchase, but you have to remember)
- **Deadline or season:** モッピー: points can be forfeited if you earn nothing for 180 days. ポイントインカム: auto-withdrawal after 180 days without use (secondary). ハピタス: exchange limit 30,000pt a month.
- **Traps and caveats:** ad blockers and cookie settings break tracking. Apps (vs browser) often don't track. "Earn by watching ads / easy tasks" schemes and side-job pitches are the scam version: the 国民生活センター warned on May 19–20, 2026 about "簡単なタスクで稼げる" side-job trouble and operators who make victims borrow money. Never pay to join. Legitimate point sites are free and use 1:1 exchanges to recognised point programmes. Industry body JIPC membership is claimed by some sites (secondary only; no current member list found). The time cost is real: a few hundred yen a month from routing is easy to lose to impulse buys the site encourages.
- **Why people miss it:** it feels too small to bother with, while the large one-off offers (¥10k–¥27k per card or account) on the same sites go unnoticed
- **What we may say:** we can explain the mechanism, show the published exchange rates and a user's own logged earnings. We should not imply guaranteed earnings.
- **Freelancer paid from abroad:** nothing. Business purchases (software, hardware) routed through a point site still count as business expenses at the price paid. Treatment of the points is unknown, verify with a 税理士.
- **Sources:** モッピー https://pc.moppy.jp/ (Oct 31, 2026: "1P＝1円", "手数料無料", no expiry but 180-day inactivity forfeiture); ハピタス exchange https://hapitas.jp/exchange/ (Oct 31, 2026); ポイントインカム (secondary) https://haruwari.com/pointincome-reputation/ and https://minhyo.jp/pointi; 国民生活センター もうけ話 page https://www.kokusen.go.jp/soudan_now/data/moukebanashi.html (updated May 20, 2026); JIPC (secondary, 2007–2011 reports) https://paymentnavi.com/cardnavi/18583.html
- **Confidence:** verified primary for the モッピー and ハピタス rates and fees and the 国民生活センター warnings. Secondary for ポイントインカム. The typical routing rebate rate is unknown, verify.

### Paying taxes by card, smartphone or auto-debit (税金のクレカ・スマホ納付、振替納税)

- **Group:** quick money
- **Who it is for:** anyone paying income tax, consumption tax, resident tax or car tax directly, especially freelancers
- **What it is:** national tax can be paid by card through the 国税クレジットカードお支払サイト for a fee, or by smartphone pay apps for amounts up to ¥300,000. Local taxes can be paid by card through eLTAX's eLお支払サイト for a smaller fee. The fee usually eats most of the card points, so the real wins are hitting a card's sign-up spending threshold and a few weeks of float.
- **Value:** points earned − fee. National tax card fee: ¥99 per ¥10,000 band on the NTA Q&A (about 0.99%); the payment site may now charge less (¥83 seen in a search snippet, unverified). Example: ¥300,000 income tax by card costs ¥2,970 (at ¥99) and earns ¥3,000 at 1%, so about zero. Local tax: ¥40 up to ¥10,000, then ¥82–83 per extra ¥10,000 (about 0.8%). Better: **振替納税** (auto-debit) for income tax took money on Apr 23, 2026 instead of the Mar 16 deadline, about 5 weeks of free float.
- **Time to money:** days (points) / weeks (float)
- **Risk:** none
- **Effort:** minutes
- **Deadline or season:** 確定申告 season (Feb 16–Mar 16, 2026 for the 2025 tax year); 振替納税 must be applied for by the payment deadline
- **Traps and caveats:** some cards give reduced or no points on tax and public payments (verify per card). The fee is not refunded if the payment is cancelled (verify). Paying tax by card while carrying a revolving balance is a disaster. Smartphone app payments may earn no points (verify per app).
- **Why people miss it:** people assume "pay tax by card = free points" and never do the fee arithmetic, or don't know a card sign-up bonus can be unlocked with a tax payment
- **What we may say:** fee and points arithmetic with the user's numbers is fine
- **Freelancer paid from abroad:** your tax bills are larger and lump-sum (income tax, 予定納税, consumption tax if registered), so they are the best tool for hitting a new card's spending threshold.
- **Sources:** NTA credit card payment Q&A https://www.nta.go.jp/taxes/tetsuzuki/shinsei/nofu-shomei/nofu/credit_nofu/credit_qa.htm (Oct 31, 2026; table ¥99 per ¥10k band, page partly undated); NTA cashless payment page https://www.nta.go.jp/taxes/shiraberu/shinkoku/tokushu/cashless-nouhu/ (Oct 31, 2026; app limit ¥300,000, 振替納税 Apr 23/30, 2026); 島根県 eLTAX fee table https://www.pref.shimane.lg.jp/life/zei/ken/nozei/credit.html (Oct 31, 2026); kokuzei.noufu.jp (refused connection; the ¥83 figure comes only from a search snippet)
- **Confidence:** verified primary for the local-tax fees, the app limit and the 振替納税 dates. National card fee: primary (¥99) but possibly outdated, verify on the payment site's simulator.

### Prepay 国民年金 up to two years (国民年金保険料の前納)

- **Group:** quick money
- **Who it is for:** 国民年金 Category 1 members: freelancers, the self-employed, students and the unemployed aged 20–59
- **What it is:** if you pay pension contributions in advance, the government gives a discount. Two years in advance saves the most, and you can pay by card to earn points on top.
- **Value:** discount + card points − interest you would have earned on the cash. FY2026 monthly premium ¥17,920. 2-year prepayment saves **¥17,370 by bank debit** (¥417,150 paid) or **¥16,010 by card or cash** (¥418,510). 1-year saves ¥4,510 by debit / ¥3,820 by card; 6-month saves ¥1,220 / ¥870. The early-debit option (早割) saves ¥60 a month. Example: card 2-year ¥16,010 + 1% points ¥4,185 = **about ¥20,200**, against ¥17,370 by debit. Note that ¥418,510 at aozora's 1.2% would earn about ¥5,000 a year before tax, so the edge is modest but positive.
- **Time to money:** months to years (the discount is built in)
- **Risk:** none (low liquidity: about ¥418k tied up)
- **Effort:** minutes (one form)
- **Deadline or season:** to start the 2-year card prepayment in April 2027, the form must **reach the 年金事務所 by end of February 2027**. Cash 2-year: apply Feb 1 to end of March, pay by end of April.
- **Traps and caveats:** you can't pay by card if you have a partial exemption. Card point rates on public payments may be reduced (verify per card). You can claim the tax deduction for the whole prepayment in the year paid or spread it across the years covered (general rule; not confirmed on the pension page, verify with NTA). If you join a company (厚生年金) mid-period, the unused part is refunded (verify).
- **Why people miss it:** the default bill is monthly and the 2-year form has a hard February deadline
- **What we may say:** discount arithmetic with the user's numbers is fine
- **Freelancer paid from abroad:** directly relevant; this is the core freelancer pension. A big prepayment also helps hit card spending thresholds.
- **Sources:** 日本年金機構 前納 https://www.nenkin.go.jp/service/kokunen/hokenryo/zenno.html; 2年前納 https://www.nenkin.go.jp/service/kokunen/hokenryo/ninenzenno.html; card payment https://www.nenkin.go.jp/service/kokunen/hokenryo/creditcard.html (all Oct 31, 2026)
- **Confidence:** verified primary

### Add 付加年金 (付加保険料)

- **Group:** insurance & benefits
- **Who it is for:** 国民年金 Category 1 and voluntary members under 65 who are not exempt and not in 国民年金基金
- **What it is:** pay an extra ¥400 a month, and your pension goes up by ¥200 a year for every month you paid, for life. After two years of receiving the pension, you have got all your money back; everything after that is profit.
- **Value:** cost = ¥400 × months; extra pension per year = ¥200 × months. Example: 10 years paid = ¥48,000, giving +¥24,000 a year from age 65, so it pays back by 67. 40 years paid gives +¥96,000 a year.
- **Time to money:** years (from pension age)
- **Risk:** none (not inflation-indexed, verify)
- **Effort:** minutes (a form at the city office or pension office, or online)
- **Deadline or season:** starts from the month you apply; it cannot be backdated
- **Traps and caveats:** you can't combine it with 国民年金基金. It stops if you get an exemption.
- **Why people miss it:** ¥400 sounds trivial and nobody advertises it
- **What we may say:** formula and payback with the user's numbers is fine
- **Freelancer paid from abroad:** directly relevant
- **Sources:** 日本年金機構 付加保険料 https://www.nenkin.go.jp/service/kokunen/hokenryo/fukanofu.html and https://www.nenkin.go.jp/service/kokunen/hokenryo/hokenryo.html (Oct 31, 2026)
- **Confidence:** verified primary

### Park cash in a high-interest online bank (高金利ネット銀行の普通預金)

- **Group:** invest & cash
- **Who it is for:** anyone holding an emergency fund or tax money in yen
- **What it is:** Japanese savings rates have risen. Some online banks pay 2–3× what a default account pays, on instant-access accounts protected by deposit insurance (up to ¥10M per person per bank; verify on dic.go.jp).
- **Value:** balance × (high rate − current rate) × (1 − 20.315% tax). Example: ¥1,000,000 at あおぞら銀行 BANK 1.2% earns ¥12,000 a year before tax, against ¥4,000 at 楽天銀行's standard 0.40%. That is **¥8,000 more before tax (about ¥6,375 after)**. 楽天銀行 with マネーブリッジ (linked to 楽天証券) pays 0.48% up to ¥10M.
- **Time to money:** monthly or semi-annual interest
- **Risk:** none (variable rate)
- **Effort:** an evening
- **Deadline or season:** rates are variable. あおぞら's 1.2% has applied since Oct 1, 2026. A secondary source says the rate above ¥1M is 0.75%; the bank's top page shows only "1.2% up to ¥1M".
- **Traps and caveats:** headline rates often carry conditions (a securities link, salary deposit, a balance cap). Teaser 定期 campaigns may require new money. Watch for transfer-out and ATM fees, which can eat the interest on small balances.
- **Why people miss it:** after decades of 0.001%, people stopped checking
- **What we may say:** rate comparison and arithmetic is fine. Deposits are not investment products, so this is low regulatory risk. Avoid "best bank for you" wording if paid by the bank.
- **Freelancer paid from abroad:** good place for the yen you set aside for tax and 国民年金. Check whether the bank accepts incoming foreign remittances and what it charges if PayPal or Wise pay you out there (unknown, verify).
- **Sources:** あおぞら銀行 top page https://www.aozorabank.co.jp/ (Oct 31, 2026: "普通預金年1.2％", "2026年10月1日現在の適用利率（変動金利）、税引前"); 楽天銀行 rates https://www.rakuten-bank.co.jp/interest/details.html (Oct 31, 2026); tier above ¥1M (secondary) https://everyday-output.com/posts/futsuyokin-ginko-hikaku-2026/ and https://money-lifehack.com/article/6966
- **Confidence:** verified primary for both banks' headline rates. Secondary for the あおぞら tier above ¥1M and the 20.315% tax rate.

## Stop the leak

### Switch off revolving credit (リボ払い・自動リボ)

- **Group:** stop the leak
- **Who it is for:** every credit card holder in Japan
- **What it is:** revolving credit lets you pay a small fixed amount each month while the rest of your balance gathers interest at 15–18% a year. Some settings make every purchase revolving automatically (三井住友's マイ・ペイすリボ turns all one-payment purchases into revolving). Some cards are revolving-only (verify per card). Issuers sometimes give points for signing up.
- **Value:** interest avoided. Rates: 楽天カード 17.64% APR (15.00% on Black/Premium). 三井住友カード 18.0% at sign-up for new members from Mar 31, 2026 (15.0% for cards opened or revolving limits set on or before Mar 30, 2026; preferential 9.8–18.0% later). Worked example, ¥300,000 balance (our simple monthly-interest calculation): at 15%, paying ¥10,000 a month takes 38 months and **¥78k interest**; at 17.64%, ¥10,000 a month takes 40 months and **¥99k**; at 17.64%, ¥6,000 a month takes 92 months and **¥246k**. 楽天's minimum is ¥3,000 a month up to ¥200,000 of balance, which barely covers interest at 17.64% (about ¥2,940 a month on ¥200k).
- **Time to money:** days (stops the drain at once)
- **Risk:** none
- **Effort:** minutes: set the card's payment method to 1回払い in the member site, cancel auto-revolving registration and check for "リボ専用" cards. If you carry a balance, pay it down early with 繰上返済 (extra lump payments).
- **Deadline or season:** before the next statement closing date
- **Traps and caveats:** a low fixed payment hides a growing balance. 分割払い chosen after purchase is also 17.64% (楽天). Points-for-revolving campaigns cost far more than the points. Cash advances (キャッシング) run 18.0% (楽天). Help line: 金融庁 / 財務局 多重債務 counselling, 日本クレジットカウンセリング協会.
- **Why people miss it:** statements show a small "this month" amount, and the setting is sometimes switched on at sign-up or through a campaign
- **What we may say:** showing the user's own balance, rate and payoff time is fine and protective. Steering someone to a specific refinancing loan is credit intermediation; don't.
- **Freelancer paid from abroad:** irregular USD income tempts people to revolve between payments. Keep a yen buffer in the bank instead (see the bank row).
- **Sources:** 楽天カード revolving simulator https://www.rakuten-card.co.jp/revo/simulation/20180910/ (Oct 31, 2026); 楽天 installment page https://www.rakuten-card.co.jp/adjustment/installment/simulation/ (Oct 31, 2026); 楽天 cash-advance simulator https://www.rakuten-card.co.jp/cashing/simulation/ (Oct 31, 2026); 三井住友カード revolving page https://www.smbc-card.com/mem/revo/index.jsp (Oct 31, 2026); 金融庁 多重債務 contacts https://www.fsa.go.jp/policy/kashikin/index.html
- **Confidence:** verified primary for the rates. The ¥300k figures are our calculation (simple monthly compounding; issuers compute daily, so the real cost differs slightly). The existence of revolving-only cards is unverified.

### Installments, card loans and cash advances (分割払い・カードローン・キャッシング)

- **Group:** stop the leak
- **Who it is for:** anyone tempted to spread a purchase or borrow short-term
- **What it is:** splitting a purchase into many payments, or borrowing on a card or loan card, costs 12–18% a year. Two payments (2回払い) is usually free; more than that is not.
- **Value:** interest avoided ≈ amount × APR × average months outstanding / 12. Legal caps under 利息制限法: 15–20% by loan size. Lenders (貸金業者) cannot lend more once your balance passes one-third of your annual income (総量規制; bank loans are excluded). 楽天カード installments: 12.25–15.00% if chosen at checkout, 17.64% if changed afterwards; 2回払い free. Example: ¥200,000 laptop in 12 installments at 15% costs roughly ¥16,600 in interest (our approximation: ¥200k × 15% × 6.5/12).
- **Time to money:** days
- **Risk:** none (avoiding it)
- **Effort:** minutes
- **Deadline or season:** none
- **Traps and caveats:** "金利0%" installment campaigns are fine only if truly 0 and paid on time. Loan offers sit alongside self-back offers (A8 lists 楽天銀行スーパーローン and フクホー); applications count against you on the credit file for 6 months. Unregistered lenders (ヤミ金) are illegal; the 金融庁 has a registered-lender search.
- **Why people miss it:** "only ¥X a month" framing
- **What we may say:** cost arithmetic is fine. Recommending a specific loan is not.
- **Freelancer paid from abroad:** business equipment is better bought outright and depreciated. Ask a 税理士 about 少額減価償却 (verify).
- **Sources:** 金融庁 貸金業法のキホン https://www.fsa.go.jp/policy/kashikin/kihon.html (Oct 31, 2026); 楽天カード installments https://www.rakuten-card.co.jp/adjustment/installment/simulation/ (Oct 31, 2026); 楽天 cash-advance simulator https://www.rakuten-card.co.jp/cashing/simulation/ (Oct 31, 2026)
- **Confidence:** verified primary for the rates and caps. The example is our approximation.

### Buy-now-pay-later and convenience-store bill fees (BNPL・あと払い: Paidy など)

- **Group:** stop the leak
- **Who it is for:** anyone using Paidy, au PAY あと払い, メルペイスマート払い and similar
- **What it is:** BNPL bundles a month's purchases into one bill. Paying by direct debit is free, but paying at a convenience store adds a fee per bill, and late payment adds charges and can affect future use.
- **Value:** fees avoided. Paidy: direct debit free; convenience-store payment ¥390 per one-payment bill; installment plans charge ¥109–¥371 per payment at a convenience store. Example: ¥390 × 12 months = **¥4,680 a year** for the habit of paying at a convenience store.
- **Time to money:** days
- **Risk:** none
- **Effort:** minutes (switch to 口座振替)
- **Deadline or season:** monthly due dates
- **Traps and caveats:** Paidy's late fee and default interest rate: **unknown, verify** (Paidy has a separate "遅延手数料について知りたい" article we could not retrieve). Several BNPLs stack spending across apps without a credit check you notice.
- **Why people miss it:** the fee appears at the register, not on the purchase
- **What we may say:** fee comparison is fine
- **Freelancer paid from abroad:** nothing
- **Sources:** Paidy fees article https://cs-support.paidy.com/support/solutions/articles/150000040596 (Oct 31, 2026); Paidy terms index https://terms.paidy.com (Oct 31, 2026)
- **Confidence:** verified primary for the convenience-store fees. Late fees are unknown.

### Subscription creep (サブスクの見直し)

- **Group:** stop the leak
- **Who it is for:** everyone
- **What it is:** small monthly charges (video, music, cloud storage, apps, gyms, "free trial" sign-ups) pile up. Many renew automatically, and some 定期購入 "trial" offers lock you into multi-month contracts.
- **Value:** sum of unused subscriptions × 12. Example: two forgotten ¥1,500 subscriptions = **¥36,000 a year**.
- **Time to money:** days
- **Risk:** none
- **Effort:** an evening (scan 3 months of card and app-store statements), then a quarterly check
- **Deadline or season:** before each renewal date; annual plans renew on the sign-up anniversary
- **Traps and caveats:** cancelling the app doesn't cancel the subscription; cancel in the App Store, Google Play or carrier billing. Carrier plans bundle subscriptions (for example ドコモ MAX's monthly "選べる特典"), which hides the cost. National consumer hotline: 188.
- **Why people miss it:** each charge is below the pain threshold
- **What we may say:** listing a user's recurring charges from their own data is fine
- **Freelancer paid from abroad:** USD-billed SaaS tools (Adobe, Figma etc.) on a yen card pay the card issuer's FX margin too (rate unknown, verify per card). Business subscriptions are deductible but still worth pruning.
- **Sources:** 国民生活センター theme index https://www.kokusen.go.jp/soudan_now/soudan_now.html (Oct 31, 2026; no 定期購入 page found in the index); ドコモ charge page https://www.docomo.ne.jp/charge/ (Oct 31, 2026)
- **Confidence:** secondary only (no primary statistic on Japanese subscription waste found). The example is illustrative.

### ATM and bank-transfer fees (ATM・振込手数料)

- **Group:** stop the leak
- **Who it is for:** anyone withdrawing cash at convenience stores or off-hours, or sending bank transfers from a megabank
- **What it is:** convenience-store ATMs charge ¥110–¥330 a time on many bank cards, and megabank transfers to other banks cost ¥154–¥990. Online banks and post-office ATMs often charge nothing. That adds up to thousands of yen a year.
- **Value:** fees per month × 12. Rates: ゆうちょ card at Seven/Lawson/E-net ATMs ¥220 in daytime, ¥330 otherwise. MUFG card at Seven/Lawson ¥220 weekday daytime, ¥330 nights and weekends, free on the 25th and month-end daytime. MUFG online transfer to another bank ¥154 (under ¥30k) / ¥220 (¥30k or more); counter ¥990. Example: 4 convenience-store withdrawals a month × ¥220 = **¥10,560 a year**.
- **Time to money:** days
- **Risk:** none
- **Effort:** minutes (move to a bank with free ATM and transfer allowances; withdraw at your own bank's ATMs in free hours)
- **Deadline or season:** none
- **Traps and caveats:** fee-free allowances often depend on balance or salary deposit, and change. Coin deposits carry their own fees (ゆうちょ ¥110–¥330).
- **Why people miss it:** ¥220 seems trivial at the moment of need
- **What we may say:** fee arithmetic is fine
- **Freelancer paid from abroad:** megabanks charge to receive overseas remittances (MUFG lists ¥1,500 or, for ¥8,000 and above, 0.05% with a ¥2,500 minimum, plus conversion at TTB; the table was partly garbled, verify). Receiving through PayPal or Wise and withdrawing yen domestically is usually cheaper on the receiving side; see the next row for the FX cost.
- **Sources:** ゆうちょ ATM fees https://www.jp-bank.japanpost.jp/kojin/access/atm/kj_acs_atm_goriyo.html (Oct 31, 2026, undated); MUFG ATM fees https://www.bk.mufg.jp/tesuuryou/atm/new.html (stated as of Mar 31, 2026); MUFG transfer fees https://www.bk.mufg.jp/tesuuryou/furikomi.html (Oct 31, 2026); MUFG remittance fees https://www.bk.mufg.jp/tesuuryou/gaitame.html (effective Oct 1, 2026)
- **Confidence:** verified primary. The remittance fee table needs re-checking.

### Currency-conversion spreads on foreign income (外貨両替・為替手数料)

- **Group:** stop the leak
- **Who it is for:** anyone converting foreign currency, especially freelancers paid in USD
- **What it is:** when PayPal or a bank converts your dollars to yen, it gives you a worse rate than the market rate and keeps the difference. PayPal's markup is 3–4%, which is very large on regular income. Cash exchange at banks and airports is usually worse still.
- **Value:** amount converted × (provider markup − cheaper alternative's markup). PayPal Japan: converting a balance or a transfer costs the base rate + 3.00%; converting a payment received costs the base rate + 4.00%. Receiving a commercial payment from abroad costs 3.60% + 0.50% international = 4.10%, plus a ¥40 fixed fee for yen receipts. Bank withdrawal is free for ¥50,000 or more per session (¥250 below that); instant withdrawal costs 2% (min ¥500, max ¥2,000). Example: converting $40,000 a year at +3% costs **$1,200 (about ¥180,000 at an illustrative ¥150/$)**. A mid-market-rate service such as Wise charges a published percentage instead (exact USD→JPY fee unknown, verify on wise.com).
- **Time to money:** days (next conversion)
- **Risk:** none (exchange-rate timing is a separate, market risk)
- **Effort:** an evening (set up an alternative, compare one transfer)
- **Deadline or season:** none
- **Traps and caveats:** PayPal sometimes auto-converts at the receiving moment (+4%) unless you choose to hold a USD balance (verify the account setting). Comparing headline "zero fee" offers without checking the rate spread is the classic trap. Bank cash USD rates were not available on Oct 31, 2026 (a Saturday; MUFG's table showed "----"). Spread unknown, verify.
- **Why people miss it:** the fee is hidden inside the exchange rate, not shown as a line item
- **What we may say:** we can show the user's own conversion history and the published markups. Recommending a specific remittance provider for a fee may touch 資金移動業 or intermediation rules (verify).
- **Freelancer paid from abroad:** this is the single largest leak for this user profile. Also check whether the PayPal receiving fee (4.10% + fixed) can be avoided by invoicing via bank transfer to a USD account (another file covers this; see universal-and-cross-border.md).
- **Sources:** PayPal Japan merchant fees https://www.paypal.com/jp/webapps/mpp/merchant-fees (Oct 31, 2026); Wise pricing https://wise.com/gb/pricing/send-money (Oct 31, 2026; mid-market rate stated, fee not extracted); MUFG rate tables https://www.bk.mufg.jp/ippan/kinri/list_j/kinri/kawase.html (no rates shown Oct 31, 2026)
- **Confidence:** verified primary for the PayPal markups and fees. Wise's fee and the bank cash spreads are unknown.

### Phone plan overpayment (格安SIM・オンライン専用プランへの乗り換え)

- **Group:** stop the leak
- **Who it is for:** anyone on a major carrier's main plan who uses under about 30GB a month
- **What it is:** the big three carriers' main plans cost 2–8× their own online-only brands or budget SIMs on the same networks. Switching keeps your number and usually takes an evening.
- **Value:** (current monthly − new monthly) × 12. Example: ドコモ MAX unlimited ¥8,448 → ahamo 30GB ¥2,970 saves **¥5,478 a month, about ¥65,700 a year**. ドコモ MAX 3GB ¥6,798 → IIJmio 5GB voice SIM ¥950 saves about ¥70,000 a year. Note: ahamo moves to tiered pricing on **Dec 1, 2026** (¥3,135 up to 40GB, ¥4,125 up to 60GB, ¥5,115 up to 120GB), and the ドコモ main plan fees also change Dec 1, 2026.
- **Time to money:** monthly from the next billing cycle
- **Risk:** none (lower support levels, no in-store help on online-only plans)
- **Effort:** an evening
- **Deadline or season:** before the Dec 1, 2026 ahamo price change if comparing prices; any time otherwise
- **Traps and caveats:** handset installment balances and "実質0円" device deals tie you to the carrier. Family and bundle discounts (up to ¥1,210 per line at ドコモ) may narrow the gap. Check whether you need carrier email.
- **Why people miss it:** inertia and bundled discounts that make the true price hard to see
- **What we may say:** price arithmetic with the user's bill is fine
- **Freelancer paid from abroad:** the phone bill is partly a business expense (deduct the business-use share)
- **Sources:** ahamo https://ahamo.com/ (Oct 31, 2026); ドコモ MAX https://www.docomo.ne.jp/charge/docomo_max/ (Oct 31, 2026); ドコモ charges https://www.docomo.ne.jp/charge/ (Oct 31, 2026); IIJmio ギガプラン https://www.iijmio.jp/gigaplan/ (Oct 31, 2026; 5GB ¥950)
- **Confidence:** verified primary

### Insurance you don't need (保険の見直し)

- **Group:** stop the leak
- **Who it is for:** anyone paying private medical, cancer or savings-type life insurance, especially single people with no dependants
- **What it is:** Japan's public health insurance already caps your monthly medical bill (高額療養費). Many people still pay for private medical insurance that duplicates it, or savings-type policies with low returns. Life insurance makes sense mainly when someone depends on your income.
- **Value:** premiums cut × years. Public cap example: under 70 with annual income of about ¥3.7M–¥5.1M, ¥1M of treatment costs you **about ¥93,000** for the month. Average household (2+ people) premium: **¥353,000 a year** (2024 survey). Life-insurance enrolment: 89.2% of 2+ person households, 45.6% of single households.
- **Time to money:** monthly
- **Risk:** low (it's a decision about how much risk you carry; keep an emergency fund instead)
- **Effort:** an evening
- **Deadline or season:** before the renewal or annual payment date
- **Traps and caveats:** cancelling savings-type policies early can lose principal (check the surrender value). Don't cancel before any replacement starts. Free "insurance consultation" shops earn commission and are offered as self-back items ("保険相談 about ¥10,000", anecdotal), so treat their advice as sales. 高額療養費 changes from Aug 2026 (annual cap added, monthly caps indexed) and more from Aug 2027.
- **Why people miss it:** fear-based selling and not knowing the public cap exists
- **What we may say:** we can show 高額療養費 caps and the user's own premium totals. Recommending cancelling or buying a specific policy is insurance solicitation (保険業法) territory; don't.
- **Freelancer paid from abroad:** you have no 傷病手当金 under 国民健康保険 (general rule, verify), so income-protection cover may be the one policy worth keeping. Medical cover is the duplicate one.
- **Sources:** 厚生労働省 高額療養費 https://www.mhlw.go.jp/stf/seisakunitsuite/bunya/kenkou_iryou/iryouhoken/juuyou/kougakuiryou/index.html (updated Jul 31, 2026); 生命保険文化センター 2024 survey https://www.jili.or.jp/research/report/zenkokujittai.html (Oct 31, 2026); tabinvest (self-back insurance consult, anecdotal) https://tabinvest.net/selfback-recomended/ (Aug 7, 2024)
- **Confidence:** verified primary for the caps, the 2026 revision and the premium survey. The 国保 no-sickness-allowance point is unverified here.

## Unclaimed money

### Dormant bank accounts (休眠預金)

- **Group:** unclaimed money
- **Who it is for:** anyone with old bank, post-office or 信金 accounts not touched for years (student accounts, old employer salary accounts), and heirs
- **What it is:** deposits with no activity for 10+ years (last activity on or after Jan 1, 2009) are moved into a public fund. You can still withdraw them **at any time, with no deadline**, at the original bank.
- **Value:** the balance left in each account. Example: a forgotten ¥30,000 student account returns ¥30,000. Yearly national totals: unknown, verify.
- **Time to money:** weeks (counter procedure, often slower than normal)
- **Risk:** none
- **Effort:** an evening (list old banks, visit or call each)
- **Deadline or season:** none
- **Traps and caveats:** take ID; a passbook is not required. 財形, foreign-currency deposits and マル優 accounts are not covered. Scam version: calls or texts saying "your dormant deposit can be released for a fee"; banks don't charge a release fee (general advice; verify).
- **Why people miss it:** people forget accounts after moving or changing jobs
- **What we may say:** explaining the procedure is fine
- **Freelancer paid from abroad:** nothing
- **Sources:** 金融庁 休眠預金 https://www.fsa.go.jp/policy/kyuminyokin/kyuminyokin.html (Oct 31, 2026); 預金保険機構 https://www.dic.go.jp/kyumin/index.html (returned 403 on Oct 31, 2026)
- **Confidence:** verified primary for the rules. National totals are unknown.

### Lost pension records (年金記録の確認・ねんきんネット)

- **Group:** unclaimed money
- **Who it is for:** anyone who has worked in Japan, especially people who changed jobs, names or addresses
- **What it is:** ねんきんネット shows your full pension record and lets you search records with no owner attached (持ち主不明記録). Gaps or missing employment periods lower your future pension; fixing them restores it.
- **Value:** extra future pension from restored months. Each restored month adds roughly 1/480 of the full basic pension, plus any 厚生年金 amount (full basic pension amount for FY2026: unknown, verify).
- **Time to money:** years (pension age), or immediately if you are already receiving a pension
- **Risk:** none
- **Effort:** an evening (register through マイナポータル with a My Number Card)
- **Deadline or season:** none
- **Traps and caveats:** check name variants and old employer names. Unpaid 国民年金 months can only be paid within 2 years (general rule, verify).
- **Why people miss it:** nobody reads the ねんきん定期便
- **What we may say:** explaining how to check is fine
- **Freelancer paid from abroad:** check that freelance years show as Category 1 paid months, not unpaid
- **Sources:** 日本年金機構 ねんきんネット https://www.nenkin.go.jp/denshibenri_kojin/n_net/index.html (Oct 31, 2026)
- **Confidence:** verified primary for the service. The value formula is approximate.

### Old employer DC money auto-transferred (企業型DC・自動移換)

- **Group:** unclaimed money
- **Who it is for:** anyone who left a company with a 企業型確定拠出年金 (corporate defined-contribution pension) and never moved it
- **What it is:** if you don't move your company DC pot within 6 months of leaving, it is automatically transferred to a holding body. There it is not invested, fees are taken, and the months don't count towards the period you need to qualify for withdrawals, which can delay when you can draw it.
- **Value:** fees avoided + investment growth regained. Fees listed: ¥3,300 (特定運営管理機関) and ¥1,048 (国民年金基金連合会) at auto-transfer; ¥58 + ¥40 a month thereafter (starting the 4th month; table read from the iDeCo page, verify that both apply). Moving the pot into iDeCo costs ¥550 + ¥2,829. Example: ¥500,000 sitting idle for 5 years at an illustrative 3% return gives up about ¥80,000 of growth (our arithmetic).
- **Time to money:** weeks to process; years until withdrawal age
- **Risk:** none for the move; market risk once invested
- **Effort:** an evening (open iDeCo; joining iDeCo usually triggers the transfer automatically)
- **Deadline or season:** 6 months after leaving the job
- **Traps and caveats:** a lump-sum withdrawal (脱退一時金) is only allowed under strict conditions and costs ¥4,180. Forgotten logins mean contacting the 特定運営管理機関 (via JIS&T / 国民年金基金連合会).
- **Why people miss it:** the exit paperwork arrives with dozens of other leaving documents
- **What we may say:** explaining the status and fees is fine. Choosing funds is 投資助言; don't.
- **Freelancer paid from abroad:** as a freelancer you can contribute to iDeCo up to the Category 1 limit (amount: verify, rules changing). The old DC pot should go into that same iDeCo account.
- **Sources:** iDeCo公式 転職・退職 https://www.ideco-koushiki.jp/retirement/ (Oct 31, 2026)
- **Confidence:** verified primary for the fees and the 6-month rule. The growth example is illustrative.

### High medical bills: 高額療養費 refunds and the medical expense deduction (高額療養費・医療費控除)

- **Group:** unclaimed money
- **Who it is for:** anyone with a high-cost month of treatment, or a year of medical spending over ¥100,000 (or over 5% of income if total income is under ¥2M)
- **What it is:** if one month's medical bill exceeds your income-based cap, your insurer refunds the excess (or you show a 限度額適用認定 / My Number insurance card to pay only the cap). Separately, yearly medical spending above ¥100,000 can be deducted from income in your tax return.
- **Value:** refund = bill − monthly cap (about ¥93,000 for ¥3.7M–¥5.1M income on ¥1M of treatment). Deduction = medical costs − insurance reimbursements − ¥100,000, max ¥2M; tax saved = deduction × your marginal rate (income tax + 10% resident tax). Alternative: セルフメディケーション税制, OTC drugs above ¥12,000, max deduction ¥88,000. Example: ¥250,000 net medical costs at a 20% income-tax bracket saves ¥150,000 × (20% + 10%) = **¥45,000**.
- **Time to money:** months
- **Risk:** none
- **Effort:** an evening
- **Deadline or season:** tax deduction counts expenses paid Jan 1–Dec 31. The 高額療養費 claim deadline (commonly 2 years) is not on the MHLW page; verify with your insurer.
- **Traps and caveats:** from Aug 2026 the monthly caps are indexed to medical-cost growth and a yearly cap is added; income bands are split further from Aug 2027. Keep receipts for 5 years. You must choose either the medical expense deduction or セルフメディケーション, not both.
- **Why people miss it:** some 国保 insurers require you to apply for the refund rather than paying it automatically (varies, verify)
- **What we may say:** explaining the formulas is fine. Preparing someone's return is 税理士 work.
- **Freelancer paid from abroad:** with 国民健康保険, your cap depends on last year's yen income; strong USD years raise it
- **Sources:** 厚生労働省 高額療養費 https://www.mhlw.go.jp/stf/seisakunitsuite/bunya/kenkou_iryou/iryouhoken/juuyou/kougakuiryou/index.html (updated Jul 31, 2026); NTA No.1120 医療費控除 https://www.nta.go.jp/taxes/shiraberu/taxanswer/shotoku/1120.htm (Oct 31, 2026)
- **Confidence:** verified primary, except the 2-year claim limit (unverified)

### Reclaim overpaid tax: 更正の請求, 還付申告 and withheld tax on fees (更正の請求・還付申告・源泉徴収)

- **Group:** unclaimed money
- **Who it is for:** anyone who missed deductions (medical, ふるさと納税, iDeCo, 国民年金, life insurance, 寡婦/ひとり親) in a past return, salary earners who never filed, and freelancers whose Japanese clients withheld tax
- **What it is:** if you overpaid tax, you can ask for it back for up to 5 years. Filed a return but missed something: file a 更正の請求 within 5 years of the original deadline. Never needed to file but had tax withheld: file a 還付申告 within 5 years from Jan 1 of the following year. Japanese companies withhold 10.21% from many freelance fees, which you get back in your return if it exceeds your real tax.
- **Value:** missed deductions × marginal rate, or withheld tax − actual tax. Withholding: 10.21% on fees up to ¥1M per payment; above that, (amount − ¥1M) × 20.42% + ¥102,100. Example: ¥1,200,000 of Japanese-client fees across several invoices (each under ¥1M) with ¥122,520 withheld; if your real income tax is ¥60,000, you get **¥62,520** back.
- **Time to money:** weeks to months (refunds typically 1–2 months after filing; verify)
- **Risk:** none
- **Effort:** an evening (NTA's online form-creation corner, e-Tax)
- **Deadline or season:** 5 years. Now (Oct 2026) the 2021 tax year's 還付申告 window closes **Dec 31, 2026** (5 years from Jan 1, 2022; our date arithmetic, verify). Deductions that must be claimed in the original return cannot be added later.
- **Traps and caveats:** a 更正の請求 cannot be filed if the final tax doesn't change. Expect a review. "Refund agents" who take a percentage are unnecessary.
- **Why people miss it:** people think filing is over once March passes
- **What we may say:** explaining the time limits and arithmetic is fine. Preparing or advising on a specific person's return is restricted to 税理士 (税理士法).
- **Freelancer paid from abroad:** US clients don't withhold Japanese tax, so this applies to your Japanese clients. Check that no US withholding was taken from you; if it was, a W-8BEN and treaty relief apply (handled in universal-and-cross-border.md; verify).
- **Sources:** NTA No.2026 更正の請求 https://www.nta.go.jp/taxes/shiraberu/taxanswer/shotoku/2026.htm; NTA No.2030 還付申告 https://www.nta.go.jp/taxes/shiraberu/taxanswer/shotoku/2030.htm; NTA No.2795 withholding rates https://www.nta.go.jp/taxes/shiraberu/taxanswer/gensen/2795.htm; NTA No.2792 fee categories https://www.nta.go.jp/taxes/shiraberu/taxanswer/gensen/2792.htm (all Oct 31, 2026)
- **Confidence:** verified primary for the time limits and rates. That design fees are a withholding category is not confirmed: No.2792 lists "representative" items and does not name design, so verify in 源泉徴収のあらまし.

### Small-business subsidies for freelancers (小規模事業者持続化補助金・デジタル化・AI導入補助金)

- **Group:** unclaimed money
- **Who it is for:** sole proprietors and small companies with a 開業届 (持続化: "small business" defined by employee count, commonly ≤5 for commerce and services; verify in the guidelines)
- **What it is:** the government reimburses part of the cost of sales and marketing projects (持続化補助金: website, ads, equipment) or of buying software and IT tools (デジタル化・AI導入補助金, formerly IT導入補助金). You pay first, then claim. The application involves a business plan and, for 持続化, a support letter from the local chamber of commerce (商工会・商工会議所).
- **Value:** eligible spend × subsidy rate, up to the cap. デジタル化・AI導入補助金2026 通常枠: 1/2 of cost (2/3 if a minimum-wage condition is met), ¥50k–under ¥1.5M for 1+ processes, ¥1.5M–¥4.5M for 4+ processes; the tool must be pre-registered by an IT vendor. 持続化補助金 round 20: rate and cap **unknown, verify in 公募要領 第8版** (earlier rounds were widely reported as 2/3 up to ¥500k, with add-ons for インボイス and wage increases; unverified). Example: ¥300k of eligible software at 1/2 returns **¥150k**.
- **Time to money:** months to a year (paid after the project and its report)
- **Risk:** low (you pay upfront; rejection is common)
- **Effort:** a weekend or more
- **Deadline or season:** 持続化 round 20: accepting from **Nov 5, 2026**, 様式4 (support letter) by **Dec 4, 2026**, applications close **Dec 15, 2026 17:00**. デジタル化・AI導入補助金2026: final 交付申請 deadline **Jan 7, 2027 17:00** (POS and smart-register types open Jan 2027).
- **Traps and caveats:** you need a gBizID account (takes time). Consultants charging success fees of 10–20% are common (anecdotal). Things bought before approval generally don't qualify. IT vendors pushing overpriced tools are a known problem; never let a vendor "refund" your share (that is fraud).
- **Why people miss it:** freelancers assume subsidies are for companies, and the guideline PDFs are long
- **What we may say:** listing programmes, deadlines and published rates is fine. Writing the application for a fee is administrative-scrivener (行政書士) work (verify).
- **Freelancer paid from abroad:** foreign-currency sales still count as business income; eligibility is based on being a Japan-registered sole proprietor
- **Sources:** 持続化補助金 round 20 site https://r6.jizokukahojokin.info/ (Oct 31, 2026); guidelines PDF https://r6.jizokukahojokin.info/doc/r6_koubover8_ip20.pdf (downloaded but not readable by our tools); デジタル化・AI導入補助金 https://it-shien.smrj.go.jp/, 通常枠 https://it-shien.smrj.go.jp/applicant/subsidy/normal/, deadline notice https://it-shien.smrj.go.jp/news/40319 (all Oct 31, 2026)
- **Confidence:** verified primary for the デジタル化 rates and deadlines and the 持続化 dates. The 持続化 cap, rate and employee threshold are unverified.

### Local government cash benefits (自治体の給付金: 例 東京都018サポート)

- **Group:** unclaimed money
- **Who it is for:** residents, depending on the municipality. Example: Tokyo residents with children aged 0–18, no income limit.
- **What it is:** cities and prefectures pay their own benefits on top of national ones: child payments, rent help, moving subsidies, energy-saving appliance rebates. Most require you to apply; nothing arrives automatically.
- **Value:** benefit × eligible months. Example: 018サポート pays ¥5,000 per child per month (**¥60,000 a year**), paid in August, December and April.
- **Time to money:** months
- **Risk:** none
- **Effort:** an evening (My Number Card and a 公金受取口座 make it faster)
- **Deadline or season:** 018サポート FY2026: application for the December payment closes **Nov 1, 2026**; April 2027 payment (and final FY2025 deadline) **Mar 1, 2027**
- **Traps and caveats:** benefits vary by ward and city and change yearly. Scam calls impersonate city offices about "給付金" and ask for ATM operations; real offices never do that (general advice, verify).
- **Why people miss it:** each ward publishes its own list and nobody aggregates it
- **What we may say:** listing programmes and deadlines is fine
- **Freelancer paid from abroad:** low or uneven yen income can make you eligible for income-tested local benefits; eligibility uses taxable income from your 確定申告 (general rule, verify)
- **Sources:** 東京都 018サポート https://018support.metro.tokyo.lg.jp/ (Oct 31, 2026)
- **Confidence:** verified primary for 018サポート. Other municipal programmes are not researched.
