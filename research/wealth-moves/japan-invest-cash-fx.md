# Japan: investing, cash buffer, foreign-currency income

Scope: a Japan resident (Tokyo), freelance designer, about ¥6M a year invoiced in USD to US clients and paid through PayPal. Checked Oct 31, 2026. Worked examples use USD/JPY ≈ 157.6, the mid-market rate on Wise's converter on Oct 31, 2026 (https://wise.com/jp/currency-converter/usd-to-jpy-rate). So ¥6M ≈ $38,070.

## Summary: the three moves worth most to a ¥6M, USD-paid freelancer

1. **Stop paying about 7% to get USD into yen.** At PayPal Japan's published rates, the stack on $38k a year comes to roughly **¥420k a year**: 3.6% + 0.5% cross-border + $0.30 per payment, then 3% over the base rate to convert to JPY on withdrawal.
2. Move repeat US clients to US account details (Wise ACH, which is free to receive, conversion from 0.27%) or to a bank wire into a USD account (Sony Bank: ¥0 to receive, ¥0.04–0.15 per $). Keep PayPal for clients who need it, and build its fee into the rate. **Rough gain: ¥300–400k a year.**
3. **Use the tax wrappers, in this order: iDeCo, then NISA.** From Dec 1, 2026, a freelancer can put up to ¥75,000 a month into iDeCo. At a 20% income-tax bracket that is about **¥270k a year less tax** (≈30% combined marginal rate). That cap is shared with 国民年金基金 and 付加保険料.
4. NISA growth is tax-free. ¥30k a month at 5% for 30 years ≈ ¥25.0M, of which ≈¥14.2M is gain, so it avoids **≈¥2.9M** of 20.315% tax.
5. **Size a real buffer and stop parking it at 0%.** A buffer is 6–12 months of spending plus a separate pot for tax and 国保/年金. USD sitting in PayPal or Wise earns 0%. Megabanks pay 0.5% from Nov 2, 2026, while CPI is +2.1%.
6. For the buffer, Aozora BANK pays 1.2% on the first ¥1M, and 個人向け国債 変動10年 pays 2.05% for the Oct 2026 issue (cash it in after year 1 with a small penalty). On ¥3M that is **≈¥20–50k a year more interest after tax**, plus a smaller real-value loss.
7. Cheap extras: 付加年金 (¥400 a month buys ¥200 × months of extra pension a year, so it pays back in 2 years). 国民年金 2年前納 saves ¥17,370. Deduct a spouse's 国民年金 premiums on your own return.
8. The insurance gap that matters: 国保 has no guaranteed sick pay (傷病手当金 is optional under 国民健康保険法 §58). That makes income protection worth more to this freelancer than medical riders, because 高額療養費 already caps hospital bills.
9. Compliance line: showing the user's own numbers and general rules is education. Paid, individual "buy this or sell that" on securities is 投資助言 (金商法 §2(8)(xi), registration under §29). See the last section.
10. Figures marked "verify" were not confirmed on a primary page. The main ones are the exact Wise USD→JPY fee, Revolut Japan, MRF yields, and PayPal's USD-withdrawal options for Japan accounts.

### Cut PayPal's two-layer cost on USD invoices (PayPal 手数料と通貨換算の見直し)

- **Group:** get paid better
- **Who it is for:** Japan-resident PayPal business or premier account holders who receive USD from abroad and withdraw to a Japanese bank.
- **What it is:** PayPal takes a receiving fee on every commercial payment, with an extra percentage when the payer is abroad. It then takes a second cut, 3% above its base rate, when the USD becomes yen. Most people only notice the first. Both layers can be reduced: fewer, larger invoices; withdrawals of ¥50,000 or more; building the fee into the price; and moving willing clients to cheaper rails (see the next two moves).
- **Value:** Formula: yearly USD × (3.6% + 0.5%) + $0.30 × number of payments, plus (USD left) × 3% conversion. Example: $38,070 a year in 19 invoices of $2,000. Fees ≈ $1,567 and conversion ≈ $1,095, so ≈ $2,662 ≈ **¥419k (7.0%)**. The number of invoices barely matters ($500 invoices: ¥422k). The percentage layers dominate. Inputs: annual USD, invoice count, withdrawal pattern.
- **Time to money:** days (applies from the next invoice)
- **Risk:** low (clients may resist changing how they pay)
- **Effort:** an evening
- **Deadline or season:** none. Fee table last updated Jan 22, 2026 per PayPal's page.
- **Traps and caveats:** Withdrawals under ¥50,000 cost ¥250 each. Instant withdrawal costs 2% (min ¥500, max ¥2,000). A refund received more than 1 day after the payment is converted at 4% over base. The fee page does not say whether a Japan account can withdraw USD to a Japanese bank: unknown, verify (PayPal's help pages were bot-blocked to our fetcher). A search snippet of PayPal JP help FAQ1189 says a US or Japanese bank account can be linked, and that the minimum withdrawal to a Japanese bank is ¥251. Whether incoming USD is auto-converted or held as a USD balance depends on account settings: verify in the account. Before adding a "PayPal surcharge" line, check PayPal's user agreement on surcharging (not verified). "PayPal" phishing emails asking you to "release" a held payment are a common scam.
- **Why people miss it:** The conversion cost is hidden inside the exchange rate, not shown as a fee line.
- **What we may say:** We may show the user's own fee total computed from PayPal's published table, and general ways to lower it. We should not tell them which provider to switch to in a way that earns us a referral fee without checking 資金移動業 or intermediary rules.
- **Freelancer paid from abroad:** This is the core move.
- **Sources:** PayPal Japan fee page https://www.paypal.com/jp/webapps/mpp/merchant-fees (3.60% + ¥40 domestic; +0.50% cross-border; USD fixed fee $0.30; conversion 3.00% for "その他の支払いの受け取り、銀行口座への振替"; 4.00% for late refunds; withdrawal free at ¥50,000 or more, ¥250 below; instant 2% min ¥500 max ¥2,000; "最終更新日 2026年1月22日"), checked Oct 31, 2026. PayPal JP help https://www.paypal.com/jp/cshelp/article/faq1189 (search snippet only, page blocked).
- **Confidence:** verified primary for the fee table. PayPal's grouping says markets are grouped for the cross-border rate, but only +0.50% is shown, so verify that it applies to US payers. USD-withdrawal options are unknown.

### Give US clients USD account details instead of a PayPal link (Wise の米ドル口座情報で受け取る)

- **Group:** get paid better
- **Who it is for:** Japan residents with a Wise account registered to a Japanese address, whose US clients can pay by ACH (US domestic bank transfer).
- **What it is:** Wise gives you US bank details, so a US client pays you as if you were a US supplier. Receiving by ACH is free. Converting USD to yen costs a small, visible fee from about 0.27%, and the yen arrive in your Japanese bank.
- **Value:** Formula: annual USD × (PayPal's ~7% − Wise conversion %). Example: on $38,070 at an assumed 0.5% conversion fee, the cost is ≈ ¥30k against PayPal's ≈ ¥419k, **saving ≈ ¥390k a year** if every client switches. Half the clients switching saves ≈ ¥195k. Inputs: share of income that can move, Wise's quoted fee on the day.
- **Time to money:** weeks (open account, client onboarding)
- **Risk:** low (provider and limit risk, see caveats)
- **Effort:** an evening
- **Deadline or season:** none
- **Traps and caveats:** **Japan holding limit: ¥1M total across currencies by default, raisable to ¥20M.** Each withdrawal, send from balance, or top-up is capped at ¥1M equivalent, so $38k a year means frequent conversions or raising the limit. USD domestic wires carry a receiving fee (wire into the CFSB account: 6.11 USD per deposit; only ACH is free). USD receipts have limits on CFSB details. Large receipts can trigger questions about purpose and payer. A Wise balance is not a bank deposit, so 預金保険 does not apply (funds are safeguarded under 資金移動業 rules). Whether a personal Wise account may be used for business receipts, and whether Wise Business is available to Japan sole traders: verify. The exact USD→JPY fee is shown only in the app ("0.27%以上" on the pricing page). Fake "Wise" invoices are a known scam.
- **Why people miss it:** US clients default to PayPal, and freelancers assume a non-US person cannot get US bank details.
- **What we may say:** We may compare costs using published fees and the user's volumes. We should present this as one of several routes, not a recommendation of a specific provider for a fee.
- **Freelancer paid from abroad:** Directly relevant. Keep PayPal for clients who insist, since card payments and buyer protection are reasons some clients prefer it.
- **Sources:** Wise receiving fees and limits https://wise.com/ja/help/articles/2978013/ ("国内電信送金の場合、受取手数料がかかる通貨は米ドル（USD）のみ"; "米ドル（USD）での ACH送金（ダイレクトデポジット）は無料"). Wise JP pricing https://wise.com/jp/pricing/receive (USD wire 6.11 USD per deposit) and https://wise.com/jp/pricing/ (conversion "0.27%以上"). Japan limits https://wise.com/ja/help/articles/7uZLhC448xJgbX92GYoIMe/ (holding ¥1M default, up to ¥20M; ¥1M per withdrawal, top-up, or balance send). Wise JP blog on US account details https://wise.com/jp/blog/opening-american-bank-account-from-japan. All checked Oct 31, 2026.
- **Confidence:** verified primary for receiving rules and limits. The exact USD→JPY fee is unknown (0.5% is an assumption). Third-party sites report effective cost around 1.2% from the mid rate, which is anecdotal.

### Take large invoices by bank wire into a USD account (外貨預金口座で被仕向送金を受ける)

- **Group:** get paid better
- **Who it is for:** Anyone with a Japanese bank that accepts incoming foreign-currency wires into a 外貨普通預金. Best for invoices of a few thousand dollars or more.
- **What it is:** The client sends a SWIFT wire in USD to your USD account at a Japanese bank. Sony Bank charges ¥0 to receive USD (intermediary banks may deduct a fixed amount), and converting costs ¥0.04–0.15 per dollar depending on status. The client usually pays their own bank's outgoing wire fee.
- **Value:** Formula: USD × (yen cost per $ ÷ rate) + intermediary deduction. Example: $38,070 converted at ¥0.15 per $ ≈ ¥5.7k, plus perhaps $10–25 per wire in intermediary fees (unknown, verify). Against PayPal's ≈ ¥419k, that **saves roughly ¥380–400k a year** if all income moved.
- **Time to money:** weeks (account opening)
- **Risk:** low (FX risk while you hold USD)
- **Effort:** an evening
- **Deadline or season:** none. New Sony accounts get ¥0 FX cost on yen→foreign purchases until the end of the month after next.
- **Traps and caveats:** Intermediary bank fees are deducted unpredictably. Clients may balk at wire fees on small invoices. 外貨預金 is not covered by 預金保険 (deposit insurance covers yen deposits). Banks may ask the purpose of each incoming remittance and request invoices. Other banks charge receiving fees, so check before giving out details.
- **Why people miss it:** People assume wires are expensive, which is true for small amounts and not for large ones.
- **What we may say:** Show published costs side by side with the user's invoice sizes. Do not rank banks as "best for you" in exchange for referral fees.
- **Freelancer paid from abroad:** Directly relevant.
- **Sources:** Sony Bank fees https://sonybank.jp/guide/fee/02.html (USD 為替コスト: base 15銭, Silver 10銭, Gold 7銭, Platinum 4銭; 被仕向送金 "何度でも0円", relay-bank charges deducted). Sony Bank 外貨預金 https://sonybank.jp/products/fc/. FSA 休眠預金 FAQ https://www.fsa.go.jp/policy/kyuminyokin/kyuminyokin.html (shows foreign-currency deposits sit outside the deposit-insurance-covered category). All checked Oct 31, 2026.
- **Confidence:** verified primary for Sony fees (the page shows no date). Intermediary fees are unknown.

### Hold some USD for USD bills, and convert on a schedule, not a hunch (外貨のまま支払う・分散して円転)

- **Group:** stop the leak
- **Who it is for:** Freelancers who pay USD subscriptions (design tools, fonts, hosting) or travel to the US.
- **What it is:** Converting USD to yen and later back to USD for a subscription pays the spread twice. Paying USD bills from a USD balance avoids that. For the rest, nobody can time USD/JPY. Converting a fixed share every month, or each time a payment arrives, removes the urge to gamble on the rate.
- **Value:** Formula: USD expenses × 2 × spread avoided. Example: $1,500 a year of software paid by a yen card that adds a foreign-transaction margin, versus paid from USD. The saving depends on the card's margin (unknown per card, verify). At an illustrative 2% round trip that is ≈ ¥4.7k a year: small but free.
- **Time to money:** days
- **Risk:** market (USD/JPY moves both ways while you hold USD)
- **Effort:** minutes
- **Deadline or season:** none
- **Traps and caveats:** Holding USD "waiting for a better rate" is speculation. Freelancer income is already all USD, so holding more concentrates the risk. Any gain on USD you hold and later spend or convert may be taxable (see the tax move).
- **Why people miss it:** The double conversion is invisible inside card statements.
- **What we may say:** General rule plus the user's own USD expense total. Never "convert now" or "wait for ¥X".
- **Freelancer paid from abroad:** Directly relevant.
- **Sources:** PayPal JP fee page (3% for converting balances between currencies) https://www.paypal.com/jp/webapps/mpp/merchant-fees. Sony Bank FX cost page https://sonybank.jp/guide/fee/02.html. Checked Oct 31, 2026.
- **Confidence:** secondary only for the example (the card margin is an assumption)

### Record USD income at the day's rate and track FX gains on USD you keep (外貨建取引の円換算・為替差益)

- **Group:** tax
- **Who it is for:** Japan residents receiving or holding foreign currency, which is every USD-paid freelancer.
- **What it is:** Income tax law requires each foreign-currency sale to be converted to yen at the rate on the transaction date. If you then keep the dollars and later spend or convert them at a different rate, the difference is an FX gain or loss. A gain is taxable: business-related gains go in 事業所得, while savings-type gains are usually treated as 雑所得 under 総合課税, taxed at your marginal rate, not 20.315%. Rolling a USD deposit into another USD deposit at the same bank is not a taxable event.
- **Value:** Formula: USD amount × (rate when spent or converted − rate when booked). Example: $10,000 booked at ¥150 and converted at ¥157.6 gives a ¥76k gain. At a ≈30% marginal rate that is ≈ ¥23k of tax to set aside. If the yen strengthens, it is a loss that may offset gains in the same category.
- **Time to money:** months (affects the return)
- **Risk:** behavioural (forgetting to record)
- **Effort:** ongoing
- **Deadline or season:** 確定申告, Feb 16 to Mar 15 each year.
- **Traps and caveats:** Freelancers often book income at the PayPal or bank payout rate instead of the transaction-date rate. Which rate (TTM or TTB) and whether a given gain is 事業所得 or 雑所得 depends on facts. The NTA Q&A covers FX gains or losses when USD deposits are used, but its text was garbled in our fetch: verify with a 税理士. FX margin trading (FX証拠金) is a different regime (separate 20% tax).
- **Why people miss it:** People think "it's my own money, just converted".
- **What we may say:** General tax rules and the user's own computed figures are fine as information. Return-specific tax advice for a fee is 税理士 territory (税理士法), which was not researched in depth here.
- **Freelancer paid from abroad:** Core to this user.
- **Sources:** 所得税法 §57-3(1) via e-Gov API https://laws.e-gov.go.jp/law/340AC0000000033 ("当該外貨建取引を行つた時における外国為替の売買相場により換算"). 所得税法施行令 §167-6(2) https://laws.e-gov.go.jp/law/340CO0000000096 (same-bank, same-currency redeposit "外貨建取引に該当しないものとする"). NTA 質疑応答 on using USD deposits to buy a home https://www.nta.go.jp/law/shitsugi/shotoku/02/40.htm (gain or loss recognised on withdrawal and use; text garbled in fetch). NTA No.1521 (FX margin, separate regime) https://www.nta.go.jp/taxes/shiraberu/taxanswer/shotoku/1521.htm. Checked Oct 31, 2026.
- **Confidence:** verified primary for the conversion rule and the same-bank rollover. 雑所得 versus 事業所得 classification is secondary: verify.

### Fill New NISA before taxable investing (新NISA つみたて投資枠・成長投資枠)

- **Group:** invest & cash
- **Who it is for:** Japan residents aged 18 or over on Jan 1 of the year. One account per person. From Jan 2027, minors can use つみたて投資枠 too.
- **What it is:** A tax-free investment account. You can put in up to ¥1.2M a year in つみたて投資枠 (low-cost funds the FSA has screened) plus ¥2.4M a year in 成長投資枠 (wider range). The lifetime cap is ¥18M at cost, of which at most ¥12M is growth. Gains and dividends are never taxed, there is no time limit, and selling frees up cap space the following year.
- **Value:** Formula: future gain × 20.315% that you don't pay. Example: ¥30,000 a month at 5% a year for 30 years ≈ **¥24.97M**, of which ¥10.8M is principal and ¥14.17M is gain, so ≈ **¥2.88M of tax avoided**. ¥50,000 a month for 30 years fills the ¥18M cap exactly ≈ ¥41.6M, saving ≈ ¥4.8M of tax. The 5% return is an illustration, not a forecast. Inputs: monthly amount, years, assumed return.
- **Time to money:** years
- **Risk:** market (prices can fall a lot, and losses inside NISA cannot be offset against other gains)
- **Effort:** an evening, then ongoing automatic
- **Deadline or season:** The annual cap resets Jan 1, and unused cap does not carry over. Changing institution is allowed once a year.
- **Traps and caveats:** NISA losses cannot be offset or carried forward. Banks may steer you into high-fee or monthly-distribution funds in 成長投資枠. Selling in a panic locks in losses. Cap reuse is by cost basis (簿価) and only from the next year.
- **Why people miss it:** "I'll start when I earn more." Time in the market matters more than the amount.
- **What we may say:** The caps, the arithmetic with the user's chosen amount and assumed return, and the general category ("low-cost broad index funds"). Not specific fund picks or allocation tailored for a fee.
- **Freelancer paid from abroad:** You must fund it in yen from a Japanese account, so USD must be converted first. Use the cheap routes above.
- **Sources:** FSA NISA page https://www.fsa.go.jp/policy/nisa2/about/nisa2024/index.html (¥1.2M / ¥2.4M / ¥18M / ¥12M; 18+ on Jan 1; unlimited period; minors from 2027: つみたて only, ¥600k a year, ¥6M lifetime). NTA No.1474 https://www.nta.go.jp/taxes/shiraberu/taxanswer/shotoku/1474.htm (NISA losses cannot be offset; 令和8年4月1日現在). Tax rate: NTA No.1476 (15.315% + 5% local = 20.315%) https://www.nta.go.jp/taxes/shiraberu/taxanswer/shotoku/1476.htm. Checked Oct 31, 2026.
- **Confidence:** verified primary (caps and rules). The compounding example is our own arithmetic.

### Know what "low cost" means: fund fees compound against you (信託報酬の差)

- **Group:** stop the leak
- **Who it is for:** Anyone buying investment trusts (投資信託) in Japan.
- **What it is:** Every fund charges a yearly fee (信託報酬) taken from your balance, and some also charge an upfront sales fee (販売手数料). Broad index funds sold online in Japan typically cost around 0.05–0.2% a year with no sales fee. Actively managed funds sold at bank counters often cost 1–2% a year, sometimes with a 2–3% upfront fee. For つみたて投資枠, FSA rules exclude funds with sales fees and cap index-fund trust fees. The FSA publishes a list of eligible funds (updated Oct 23, 2026).
- **Value:** Formula: the future value at (return − fee A) minus the future value at (return − fee B). Example: ¥30,000 a month for 30 years at a 5% gross return. A 0.1% fee leaves ≈ ¥24.51M and a 1.5% fee leaves ≈ ¥19.06M: **≈ ¥5.45M lost to fees**.
- **Time to money:** years
- **Risk:** market
- **Effort:** minutes (compare the fee line on the fund's 目論見書)
- **Deadline or season:** none
- **Traps and caveats:** Monthly-distribution funds (毎月分配型) pay out your own principal. Funds of funds can stack fees, so check 実質的な負担 (the total cost actually borne). The FSA trust-fee caps for つみたて投資枠 are 0.5% for domestic and 0.75% for overseas index funds (from our memory of the FSA criteria; the overview PDF could not be parsed, so verify). The fee ranges above are general market knowledge, not from a single primary page.
- **Why people miss it:** 1.5% "sounds small", and the cost is never billed as a separate line.
- **What we may say:** Explain fees and show the user's own drag calculation. Do not name "the fund to buy".
- **Freelancer paid from abroad:** nothing
- **Sources:** FSA つみたて投資枠 eligible products https://www.fsa.go.jp/policy/nisa2/about/tsumitate/target/index.html (lists updated 2026/10/23; criteria in 「つみたて投資枠対象商品の概要について」 PDF, not parsed). Checked Oct 31, 2026.
- **Confidence:** secondary only for the fee ranges and caps. The arithmetic is ours.

### Choose 特定口座 (源泉徴収あり) for taxable investing, and use 損益通算 (特定口座・損益通算・繰越控除)

- **Group:** invest & cash
- **Who it is for:** Anyone investing beyond NISA, or holding US stocks or ETFs in a broker account.
- **What it is:** In a 特定口座 with withholding, the broker computes gains and withholds the 20.315% tax, so you need not file. In a 一般口座 you calculate and file everything yourself. If you sell at a loss, you can file to offset it against other listed-stock gains and dividends that year. You can also carry it forward 3 years, but only by filing every year, including years with no sales.
- **Value:** Formula: loss used × 20.315%. Example: a ¥300k loss offset against ¥300k of gains elsewhere saves ≈ ¥61k of tax.
- **Time to money:** months (via the return)
- **Risk:** none
- **Effort:** an evening (at tax time)
- **Deadline or season:** 確定申告 each year. The withholding choice must be made before the year's first sale.
- **Traps and caveats:** Skipping one year's return breaks the carryforward chain. NISA losses don't count. Off-exchange (相対) trades are excluded. Including dividends on the return can affect 国保 premiums and other income-based charges (not researched here, verify).
- **Why people miss it:** "The broker handles tax" is true only for withholding, and only within one account.
- **What we may say:** Rules, plus the user's own tax effect computed from their statements.
- **Freelancer paid from abroad:** Freelancers file 確定申告 anyway, so adding the stock pages costs little.
- **Sources:** NTA No.1476 特定口座 https://www.nta.go.jp/taxes/shiraberu/taxanswer/shotoku/1476.htm (15.315% + 5% withheld per sale; 申告不要 option). NTA No.1474 https://www.nta.go.jp/taxes/shiraberu/taxanswer/shotoku/1474.htm (offset against dividends under 申告分離; 3-year carryforward; must file even in years with no sales). Both 令和8年4月1日現在法令等. Checked Oct 31, 2026.
- **Confidence:** verified primary

### iDeCo for the freelancer and their spouse (iDeCo・第1号の拠出限度額引上げ)

- **Group:** tax
- **Who it is for:** 第1号被保険者 (self-employed, freelancers, their non-employee spouses) aged 20 to under 60 who are not exempt from 国民年金. From Dec 1, 2026, enrolment is possible up to age 70 under conditions.
- **What it is:** A private pension account whose contributions are fully deductible from income. Growth is untaxed until withdrawal, and the money is locked until about age 60. From Dec 1, 2026, the self-employed cap rises to ¥75,000 a month, shared with 国民年金基金 and 付加保険料. A freelancer's spouse who is not an employee's dependent is also 第1号 and gets the same cap. The ¥23,000 第3号 cap only applies to dependent spouses of employees.
- **Value:** Formula: annual contribution × marginal rate (income tax × 1.021 + 10% resident tax). Example: ¥75,000 × 12 = ¥900k. At the 20% bracket (課税所得 ¥3.3M–6.949M) that is ≈ **¥274k a year less tax**, or ≈ ¥182k at the 10% bracket. A spouse with their own income gets the same on theirs.
- **Time to money:** months (via the return), but the money itself is locked for years
- **Risk:** market plus behavioural (illiquid until about 60)
- **Effort:** a weekend (application lag of about 1–2 months)
- **Deadline or season:** The new ¥75k cap applies from Dec 1, 2026. The contribution amount can be changed once a year. Contributions count for the year paid.
- **Traps and caveats:** Money is locked, so don't put buffer money here. There are account and administration fees (a small monthly charge). Withdrawals are taxed (retirement-income deductions apply, and the rules changed recently: verify). Over-contributing when income is low wastes the deduction.
- **Why people miss it:** The lock-up scares people, and "pension" sounds like a problem for when you're old.
- **What we may say:** Limits, deduction arithmetic with the user's income, and the lock-up fact. Not which plan provider or which funds to choose.
- **Freelancer paid from abroad:** Contributions must come from a yen bank account, so USD conversion comes first.
- **Sources:** iDeCo公式 https://www.ideco-koushiki.jp/start/ (current: 第1号 ¥68,000 combined with 国民年金基金 or 付加保険料; 第3号 ¥23,000). https://www.ideco-koushiki.jp/library/2022kaisei/ (from Dec 1, 2026: 第1号 ¥75,000; 第2号 ¥62,000; enrolment to under 70 with conditions; transitional to end of Nov 2029). NTA No.2260 rate table https://www.nta.go.jp/taxes/shiraberu/taxanswer/shotoku/2260.htm. Checked Oct 31, 2026.
- **Confidence:** verified primary for limits. Whether the 第3号 cap changes in Dec 2026 is not stated on the page: verify.

### Size the cash buffer for freelance income (生活防衛資金・納税資金の確保)

- **Group:** invest & cash
- **Who it is for:** Everyone, and especially freelancers with lumpy income.
- **What it is:** Keep two pots separate. (1) An emergency buffer of months of spending. A common rule of thumb is 3–6 months for employees and 6–12 months for freelancers. (2) A tax pot for 所得税 (including 予定納税), 住民税, 国保 and 国民年金, which arrive months after the income. Invest only what is beyond both.
- **Value:** Formula: monthly spending × months, plus the next 12 months of tax and social-insurance bills. Example: ¥250k a month spending × 9 months = ¥2.25M buffer. Separately, set aside the tax bill (compute from last year's return).
- **Time to money:** months (to build)
- **Risk:** behavioural
- **Effort:** an evening
- **Deadline or season:** 予定納税 in July and November. 住民税 and 国保 are billed from June.
- **Traps and caveats:** We found no Japanese regulator page that sets a specific number of months: the 3–6 and 6–12 month figures are a widespread rule of thumb (secondary). Investing the tax pot and getting caught by a market drop at bill time is the classic freelancer disaster.
- **Why people miss it:** A good month feels like spare money, but a third of it is already owed.
- **What we may say:** Rule-of-thumb ranges and the user's own spending maths. This is general education, not regulated.
- **Freelancer paid from abroad:** Keep part of the buffer in yen. A USD-only buffer exposes rent and bills to the exchange rate, and the PayPal or Wise ¥1M limits can delay access.
- **Sources:** J-FLEC (no month rule published on the pages checked) https://www.j-flec.go.jp/. NTA No.2260 https://www.nta.go.jp/taxes/shiraberu/taxanswer/shotoku/2260.htm. Checked Oct 31, 2026.
- **Confidence:** anecdotal for the month rule. Tax timing is general knowledge (verify dates with the ward office).

### Park the buffer where it earns 1%+, not 0% (高金利ネット銀行・普通預金)

- **Group:** invest & cash
- **Who it is for:** Anyone holding more than a month's spending in cash.
- **What it is:** Since the Bank of Japan began raising rates (policy rate 1.25% from Sep 18, 2026, unchanged at the Oct 30 meeting per the statement's title), ordinary deposit rates are no longer near zero, but they differ a lot. Megabanks pay 0.4%, and 0.5% from Nov 2, 2026. Aozora Bank's BANK branch pays 1.2% on the first ¥1M and 0.75% above that. Rakuten Bank pays 0.48% with マネーブリッジ (0.40% without). All deposit interest is taxed at 20.315% at source.
- **Value:** Formula: balance × rate × 0.79685. Example: ¥3M at a megabank at 0.5% ≈ ¥11.95k a year after tax. Aozora BANK (¥1M at 1.2% plus ¥2M at 0.75%) ≈ **¥21.5k a year**. Against CPI of +2.1%, ¥3M at 0.5% still loses ≈ ¥51k of purchasing power a year.
- **Time to money:** days
- **Risk:** none (yen deposits are insured up to ¥10M principal per bank per person; secondary, see sources)
- **Effort:** an evening
- **Deadline or season:** Rates are variable and change with the policy rate. Re-check after each Bank of Japan meeting (next expected Dec 2026, date unverified).
- **Traps and caveats:** Headline rates often apply only up to a cap (Aozora ¥1M) or with conditions (securities link). "Campaign" rates expire. The old "0.001%" era is over at the big banks, so the gap is now around 0.5% versus 1.2%. The bigger leak is money left at 0%: PayPal balances, Wise balances, and current accounts.
- **Why people miss it:** "Interest is meaningless in Japan" was true until 2024.
- **What we may say:** Published rates and the user's own interest maths. We may not act as a 銀行代理業 (bank agent) or 金融サービス仲介業 (financial-services intermediary) by soliciting accounts for compensation without registration.
- **Freelancer paid from abroad:** Money sitting in PayPal or Wise USD earns nothing. Sweep it on a schedule.
- **Sources:** Aozora BANK https://www.aozorabank.co.jp/bank/products/lp/special/index_mr.html (1.2% up to ¥1M, 0.75% above; "2026年10月 1日現在", pre-tax). Rakuten Bank https://www.rakuten-bank.co.jp/interest/details.html (0.40% standard; マネーブリッジ 0.48% up to ¥10M, 0.42% above; date not shown). Megabanks to 0.5% from Nov 2: Impress Watch Sep 20, 2026 https://www.watch.impress.co.jp/docs/news/2142489.html, and MUFG release https://www.bk.mufg.jp/news/news2026/pdf/news0616_1.pdf (the Aug 3 move to 0.4%; from a search snippet). Japan Post Bank to 0.5% from Nov 9 (Impress). Bank of Japan statement list https://www.boj.or.jp/mopo/mpmdeci/state_2026/index.htm ("金融市場調節方針の変更" Sep 18, 2026; Oct 30 titled "当面の金融政策運営について"). 1.25% level: Kumamoto Nichinichi/Kyodo https://kumanichi.com/articles/2037289. CPI Sep 2026 +2.1% y/y: 総務省統計局 https://www.stat.go.jp/data/cpi/sokuhou/tsuki/index-z.html (published Oct 23, 2026). Checked Oct 31, 2026.
- **Confidence:** verified primary for Aozora, Rakuten, the Bank of Japan statement list and CPI. Secondary for the 1.25% level (news) and the megabank 0.5% (news). Deposit-insurance limits are general knowledge: dic.go.jp blocked our fetch, so verify.

### Use 個人向け国債 変動10年 for the slow half of the buffer (個人向け国債)

- **Group:** invest & cash
- **Who it is for:** Japan individuals, from ¥10,000, sold monthly at banks and brokers.
- **What it is:** A government bond for individuals whose rate resets every 6 months at 0.66 × the 10-year JGB yield, with a floor of 0.05%. After 1 year you can cash it in at face value minus the last two coupons × 0.79685. It cannot lose principal from rate moves, which suits money you probably won't need for a year.
- **Value:** Formula: amount × rate × 0.79685. Example: ¥2M in the Oct 2026 issue at 2.05% ≈ **¥32.7k a year after tax** (1.6335% after tax), versus ¥8k at a 0.5% bank. Early-exit cost ≈ two coupons after tax ≈ ¥32.7k, so only use money you can leave for a while.
- **Time to money:** months (1-year lock)
- **Risk:** low (rate falls reduce income; there is an early-exit penalty)
- **Effort:** minutes
- **Deadline or season:** Monthly offerings. The Oct 2026 issue was offered Oct 8–30, issued Nov 16, 2026. Next is Nov 2026 (rate not yet published).
- **Traps and caveats:** Bank "campaigns" tie the bond to selling you other products. Not usable for emergencies in year 1. Fixed 5-year (2.39%) and fixed 3-year (2.07%) lock in a rate but don't float up.
- **Why people miss it:** Most people assume government bonds are for old people or pay 0.05%.
- **What we may say:** Published rate and terms, plus the user's interest maths.
- **Freelancer paid from abroad:** nothing (bought in yen)
- **Sources:** MOF current offering https://www.mof.go.jp/jgbs/individual/kojinmuke/recruitment/index.html (変動10年 2.05%, after tax 1.6335425%; 固定5年 2.39%; 固定3年 2.07%; offered 令和8年10月8日～10月30日; issued 令和8年11月16日; page dated 令和8年10月30日). Product terms https://www.mof.go.jp/jgbs/individual/kojinmuke/main/outline/hendou/ (×0.66, floor 0.05%, 1-year lock, 0.79685 adjustment). Checked Oct 31, 2026.
- **Confidence:** verified primary

### Don't leave payouts sitting at 0% in PayPal, Wise or MRF-by-default (残高の放置)

- **Group:** stop the leak
- **Who it is for:** Freelancers whose income lands in a payment app and stays there.
- **What it is:** Money in a PayPal or Wise balance earns no interest and is not a bank deposit. Uninvested cash at a broker usually sits in an MRF or a "deposit" sweep, whose yield varies (unknown for 2026, verify). A fixed sweep day, for example the 1st and 15th, moves balances to the buffer, the bond or NISA.
- **Value:** Formula: average idle balance × (best safe rate − 0) × 0.79685. Example: an average ¥800k idle in PayPal or Wise, moved to a 1.2% account, gives ≈ ¥7.6k a year. The bigger gain is behavioural: money not visible as "available" doesn't get spent.
- **Time to money:** days
- **Risk:** none
- **Effort:** minutes, then ongoing
- **Deadline or season:** none
- **Traps and caveats:** Sweeping PayPal often costs 3% conversion and a ¥250 fee under ¥50,000. Sweep less often, in bigger amounts, or fix the route first (see the PayPal move). Wise Japan caps the balance at ¥1M by default.
- **Why people miss it:** The balance "feels" like a bank account.
- **What we may say:** Show the user's idle balance and the yield gap. That is information.
- **Freelancer paid from abroad:** Directly relevant.
- **Sources:** PayPal JP fees https://www.paypal.com/jp/webapps/mpp/merchant-fees. Wise Japan limits https://wise.com/ja/help/articles/7uZLhC448xJgbX92GYoIMe/. Aozora rate (above). MRF: the Rakuten Securities page returned empty, so the yield is unknown. Checked Oct 31, 2026.
- **Confidence:** verified primary for fees and limits. MRF yield is unknown.

### Know what 国民年金 alone pays, and close the gap (老齢基礎年金だけの金額)

- **Group:** insurance & benefits
- **Who it is for:** Freelancers and the self-employed, who have only the basic pension unless they add to it.
- **What it is:** Forty years of full 国民年金 contributions pays **¥847,300 a year (≈ ¥70,600 a month) from age 65 in FY2026**. Missing months reduce it proportionally. Employees get 厚生年金 on top, and freelancers don't. So the gap has to be filled by iDeCo, NISA, 付加年金 or 国民年金基金. FY2026 contributions are ¥17,920 a month.
- **Value:** Formula: ¥847,300 × months paid ÷ 480. Example: 5 unpaid years (60 months) cost ≈ ¥106k a year for life. Partial-exemption months count at 4/8 to 7/8 of a paid month.
- **Time to money:** years
- **Risk:** none
- **Effort:** minutes (check ねんきんネット)
- **Deadline or season:** Missed contributions can be paid only within 2 years (general knowledge, verify). Exemptions must be applied for.
- **Traps and caveats:** Unpaid months without an exemption count as zero toward both amount and eligibility (10-year minimum). Students and low-income years should apply for 免除 or 猶予 rather than ignore the bill.
- **Why people miss it:** People assume "the pension" is like a salaried worker's.
- **What we may say:** Official amounts and the user's own record. This is information.
- **Freelancer paid from abroad:** nothing. Residence, not income currency, decides membership.
- **Sources:** 日本年金機構 https://www.nenkin.go.jp/service/jukyu/seido/roureinenkin/jukyu-yoken/20150401-02.html (令和8年4月分から 847,300円 for those born on or after Apr 2, 1956; formula with exemption weights; 付加年金 200円 × months). Premium https://www.nenkin.go.jp/service/kokunen/hokenryo/hokenryo.html (令和8年度 17,920円 a month). Checked Oct 31, 2026.
- **Confidence:** verified primary

### Pay the ¥400 付加保険料 and prepay 2 years (付加年金・2年前納)

- **Group:** insurance & benefits
- **Who it is for:** 第1号被保険者. 付加保険料 cannot be combined with 国民年金基金, and it shares the iDeCo cap.
- **What it is:** For an extra ¥400 a month you get an extra ¥200 × the number of months paid, every year for life from 65. It pays for itself after 2 years of pension. Separately, paying 2 years of 国民年金 in advance by bank debit is cheaper.
- **Value:** Formula: 付加: ¥200 × months a year of pension for ¥400 × months paid. Example: 10 years (120 months) costs ¥48,000 and adds ¥24,000 a year for life. 2年前納 by 口座振替: ¥417,150 for FY2026–27, **saving ¥17,370** (¥16,010 by cash or card).
- **Time to money:** years (付加). The 前納 discount is immediate.
- **Risk:** none (longevity: you need to live to about 67 to break even)
- **Effort:** minutes
- **Deadline or season:** Apply for 2年前納 by debit before the spring cycle. Cash applications run Feb 1 to the end of March per the page; verify the debit deadline.
- **Traps and caveats:** It uses ¥400 of the iDeCo cap. The full 2年前納 payment can be deducted in the year paid or spread, per NTA Q&A (not read here, verify).
- **Why people miss it:** Too small to notice, and not advertised.
- **What we may say:** Official numbers. This is information.
- **Freelancer paid from abroad:** nothing
- **Sources:** 日本年金機構 (付加年金 formula) https://www.nenkin.go.jp/service/jukyu/seido/roureinenkin/jukyu-yoken/20150401-02.html. 2年前納 https://www.nenkin.go.jp/service/kokunen/hokenryo/ninenzenno.html (口座振替 ¥417,150, discount ¥17,370; cash or card ¥418,510, discount ¥16,010). iDeCo shared cap https://www.ideco-koushiki.jp/start/. Checked Oct 31, 2026.
- **Confidence:** verified primary

### Deduct the spouse's (or child's) 国民年金 you pay (家族の社会保険料控除)

- **Group:** tax
- **Who it is for:** A freelancer who pays national pension or health premiums for a spouse or relative sharing the same household budget (生計を一にする), for example a student child over 20 or a low-income spouse.
- **What it is:** Social insurance premiums you actually pay for family members in your household are deductible on your own return, so the deduction lands in the highest earner's bracket.
- **Value:** Formula: premiums paid × your marginal rate. Example: spouse's ¥17,920 × 12 = ¥215k × ≈ 30% ≈ **¥65k a year**.
- **Time to money:** months (return)
- **Risk:** none
- **Effort:** minutes
- **Deadline or season:** 確定申告
- **Traps and caveats:** It must be paid by you, from your account, and not also claimed by the spouse.
- **Why people miss it:** People assume each person deducts their own premiums.
- **What we may say:** The rule plus the user's maths.
- **Freelancer paid from abroad:** nothing
- **Sources:** NTA No.1130 https://www.nta.go.jp/taxes/shiraberu/taxanswer/shotoku/1130.htm ("自己と生計を一にする配偶者やその他の親族の負担すべき社会保険料を支払った場合"; 令和8年4月1日現在). Checked Oct 31, 2026.
- **Confidence:** verified primary

### Skip medical riders, cover lost income instead (高額療養費と就業不能リスク)

- **Group:** insurance & benefits
- **Who it is for:** Young singles, especially freelancers on 国保.
- **What it is:** Public health insurance already caps monthly out-of-pocket medical costs (高額療養費). Under the August 2026 rules, the Ministry of Health's example is a 370–510万 income earner with a ¥1M bill paying about ¥93,000, with a new annual cap on top. So hospital-cash and medical riders add little. What 国保 does not reliably provide is sick pay: 傷病手当金 is optional for municipalities. A freelancer who can't work for 3 months loses income with no public replacement. Without dependents, large life cover is usually unnecessary.
- **Value:** Formula: premiums saved on unneeded medical or whole-life cover, minus the cost of income protection. Example: dropping a ¥5,000-a-month medical policy saves ¥60k a year (illustrative premium). Income protection pricing is unknown, so get quotes.
- **Time to money:** days (cancelling)
- **Risk:** low (keep the buffer)
- **Effort:** an evening
- **Deadline or season:** Check renewal dates.
- **Traps and caveats:** Don't cancel before replacement cover starts. Savings-type and foreign-currency insurance (外貨建て保険) mix insurance with investing and high hidden costs (general concern, no FSA page fetched). 高額療養費 is applied per month and per institution and has rules, and its brackets change again from Aug 2027.
- **Why people miss it:** Insurance sales target fear of hospital bills, which the public system already caps.
- **What we may say:** Explain public coverage and the user's maths. Recommending a specific policy for commission is 保険募集, regulated under 保険業法 (not researched in depth: verify).
- **Freelancer paid from abroad:** Income protection must cover yen income. Check whether insurers accept foreign-client freelance income as proof (unknown).
- **Sources:** MHLW 高額療養費 https://www.mhlw.go.jp/stf/seisakunitsuite/bunya/kenkou_iryou/iryouhoken/juuyou/kougakuiryou/index.html (Aug 2026 revision and annual cap; example ≈ ¥93,000 on ¥1M; brackets subdivided Aug 2027; updated Jul 31, 2026). 国民健康保険法 §58(2) https://laws.e-gov.go.jp/law/333AC0000000192 ("傷病手当金の支給その他の保険給付を行うことができる"). Checked Oct 31, 2026.
- **Confidence:** verified primary for the public rules. Premium examples are illustrative.

## What we may say: education vs 投資助言 (Japan)

- **Statute.** 金融商品取引法 §2(8)(xi) defines investment advice as a contract where one party agrees to advise on the value of securities or on investment decisions, orally, in writing or otherwise, and the other party pays for it. Newspapers, magazines, books and similar publications sold to the general public and freely buyable are carved out ("不特定多数の者に販売することを目的として発行されるもので、不特定多数の者により随時に購入可能なものを除く"). Doing this as a business is 投資助言・代理業 (§28(3)), which needs registration (§29). Source: e-Gov API text of law 323AC0000000025, fetched Oct 31, 2026; https://laws.e-gov.go.jp/law/323AC0000000025.
- **Safe zone for the app.** We can explain how NISA, iDeCo and bonds work. We can show published rates and fees, and calculate with the user's own numbers and the user's own chosen assumptions. We can compare costs from published tables and give general rules of thumb.
- **Risky zone.** Personalised "buy fund X", "move ¥Y into Z", or "convert USD now", given for a subscription fee. Ranking specific securities for the individual. Automated portfolio allocation.
- **Intermediation.** Sending users to a bank, broker, insurer or 資金移動業者 for a commission can be 銀行代理業, 金融商品仲介業, 金融サービス仲介業 or 保険募集, each regulated. This was not researched in depth: verify before any affiliate deals.
- **FSA guidance.** The FSA supervisory guideline for 金融商品取引業者 is at https://www.fsa.go.jp/common/law/guide/kinyushohin/index.html (page reachable). We did not extract a specific passage on apps or robo-advice: verify.
