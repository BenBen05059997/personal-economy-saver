## What holds everywhere (the cross-region patterns)

1. **The payment-channel fee stack is the single biggest line for a foreign-paid freelancer in every country checked:** ≈7% in Japan, India and the Philippines, ≈7.9% UK, ≈8% Germany. Nobody prices it in.
2. **A tax-advantaged retirement account with a deduction exists in every country** (iDeCo / 共済, Solo 401(k) / SEP, SIPP, Rürup, NPS, SSS/MP2) and is the largest recurring tax saving a freelancer has.
3. **"Free money" schemes:** government bonuses (UK LISA 25%, Help to Save 50%; Germany Altersvorsorgedepot from 2027; Japan 付加年金), employer or state matches, bank and card sign-up bonuses, self-back. The test: does someone else put money in?
4. **Revolving credit is the universal destroyer:** 18% Japan, 22.4% US average, 45% India cap, 36% Philippines cap. Paying it off beats every other move.
5. **Unclaimed money has an official search in each country** (US state unclaimed property, UK pension tracing, India UDGAM and IEPF, Japan ねんきんネット and 更正の請求 for 5 years) and nobody runs it.
6. **Cash buffer and where it sits:** rates moved in 2026 (BoJ 1.25%, US T-bills ≈4%, UK Premium Bonds 4.35%, Germany Tagesgeld 2.5%); money left at the old near-zero rate is a measurable loss.
7. **Paperwork that stops leakage:** W-8BEN for US clients (no 30% withholding), FIRA in India, LUT for GST exports, export zero-rating of services almost everywhere.

## The legal line, per region (what the app may say)

Everywhere: show the rule, the user's own numbers and the source. Never name a product to buy, never take a referral fee without a label, never classify one person's tax position.

- **Japan:** 投資助言 (金商法 §2(8)(xi), §29) for paid individual securities advice; 税理士法 for individual tax advice; 保険業法; 貸金業法; 景品表示法 stealth-marketing rule for affiliate links.
- **US:** SEC/state investment-adviser registration for personalised securities advice; state insurance licences for choosing a health plan.
- **UK:** FCA regulated advice vs guidance (PERG 8.28).
- **Germany:** Anlageberatung §2 Abs. 8 Nr. 10 WpHG; individual tax advice reserved to Steuerberater.
- **India:** SEBI IA registration; "education" framing did not protect defendants in the Dec 2024 order.
- **Philippines:** SEC advisories on promoters of unregistered investments.

## A tension to decide with the owner

Across five regions the biggest single number is the PayPal fee stack on cross-border payments. This is a PayPal-judged hackathon. The honest framing that keeps the user's interest and the judges' goodwill: "know what each payout costs, price it into your invoices, invoice in the currency that costs least, convert in bulk, and use the right PayPal product for each client." Not "leave PayPal."
