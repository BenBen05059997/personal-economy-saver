# US catalogue: verification against primary sources

Checked on **Nov 1, 2026** by opening each cited page (or its PDF) and every figure the rows and `formulas/us.ts` use. Status key: **confirmed** (matches the primary page), **corrected** (wrong or out of date, now changed), **unconfirmed-removed** (could not be confirmed, so dropped), **unconfirmed-kept-and-labelled** (kept only because the row says plainly that it is unconfirmed, anecdotal or secondary). Pages that blocked fetching (SSA, PayPal help, College Investor, IRS PDFs) were read through the built-in browser or by extracting the PDF text; none needed a sign-in.

## Conflict answers

| Question | Answer | Source |
|---|---|---|
| **2027 open enrolment: does it end Dec 15?** | **No.** HealthCare.gov open enrolment for 2027 runs **Nov 1, 2026 to Jan 15, 2027**. Dec 15 is only the last day to pick a plan for cover starting Jan 1 (later picks start Feb 1). The row's "open enrolment closes Dec 15 (reported)" was wrong; the deadline now says "last day to pick a 2027 plan for Jan 1 cover". State exchanges may differ. | https://www.healthcare.gov/quick-guide/dates-and-deadlines/ |
| **Are the enhanced subsidies back for 2027?** | **No.** KFF (Oct 30, 2026) says they expired in 2026 and does not report a restoration. The IRS's 2027 table (Rev. Proc. 2026-26) is the original-law table, top rate 10.22%, and says the failsafe exception applies. The 400% cliff therefore still applies. | https://www.kff.org/affordable-care-act/9-things-to-watch-for-the-2027-aca-open-enrollment-period/, https://www.irs.gov/pub/irs-drop/rp-26-26.pdf |
| **Free File cap: $89,000 or $95,000?** | Both are on IRS pages. The IRS newsroom for the 2026 season says **$89,000** (income for 2025). The Free File landing page, closed until January 2027, now says **$95,000**, without naming the season. The formula keeps $89,000 (confirmed, conservative); Maya's AGI is under both. | https://www.irs.gov/newsroom/2026-tax-filing-season-opens-with-several-free-filing-options-available, https://www.irs.gov/filing/irs-free-file-do-your-taxes-for-free |
| **Bank bonus tally: "about $1,000 from 5 banks"** | **Not in the cited source.** Financial Panther's 2025 post lists 20 bonuses worth $7,850 (a couple, 12 banks; the itemised amounts add to $7,950) and says 2024 was "a few hundred dollars". The Travel with Grant post reports 2024, not 2025 ($2,755 from 12 accounts, $2,030 pending). The old $1,000/yr value is dropped; the row now values one typical bonus ($300, the median of the 20). | https://financialpanther.com/back-with-the-bank-bonuses-my-2025-bank-account-bonus-earnings/, https://travelwithgrant.boardingarea.com/?p=30372 |
| **Is the first-year Solo 401(k) deadline Dec 31?** | Dec 31 is the safe date for a plan that exists. IRS Pub 560 says a sole proprietor who is the only participant and adopts a new 401(k) after year-end can have that year's elective deferrals if paid before the return due date without extensions (Apr 15, 2027). A SEP can be set up and funded up to the return due date including extensions. The row keeps 2026-12-31 and says so. | https://www.irs.gov/publications/p560 |
