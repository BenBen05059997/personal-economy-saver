| **更正の請求 / 還付申告 window for tax year 2021** | Both dates are right for different cases. A **更正の請求** on a return you filed runs 5 years from the statutory filing deadline: the 2021 return's deadline was Mar 15, 2022, so the last day is **Mar 15, 2027**. A **還付申告** (a first refund return for a year you never filed) runs 5 years from Jan 1 of the following year: for 2021 it closes **Dec 31, 2026**. | **Design work for foreign clients: 0%-rated export or 電気通信利用役務?** | **Export (0%)**, as a service to a non-resident, with the usual limits.
